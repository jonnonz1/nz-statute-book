# Dog Control (Category 1 Offences) Amendment Act 2019
_Consolidation as at 2019-12-13_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Dog Control (Category 1 Offences) Amendment Act 2019.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Dog Control Act 1996 (the principal Act).

### 4 New section 66A and cross-heading inserted
After section 66, insert:Category 1 offencesJurisdiction for category 1 offences
  (1) The District Court presided over by 2 or more Justices of the Peace has jurisdiction in respect of any category 1 offence under this Act.
  (2) Subsection (1) does not apply to the offences described in the following sections:
    (a) section 32(2):
    (b) section 57(2):
    (c) section 57A(2):
    (d) section 61(2):
    (e) section 62(4):
    (f) section 78(3).
  (3) In this section, category 1 offence has the same meaning as in section 6(1) of the Criminal Procedure Act 2011.
