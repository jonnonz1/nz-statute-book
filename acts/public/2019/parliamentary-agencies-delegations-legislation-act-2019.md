# Parliamentary Agencies Delegations Legislation Act 2019
_Consolidation as at 2019-11-16_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Parliamentary Agencies Delegations Legislation Act 2019.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date of Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Amendment to Clerk of the House of Representatives Act 1988

### 3 Amendment to Clerk of the House of Representatives Act 1988
This Part amends the Clerk of the House of Representatives Act 1988.

### 4 Section 12 amended (Delegation of functions or powers)
Replace section 12(1) with:
  (1) The Clerk of the House of Representatives may delegate any of the Clerk’s functions or powers to a person described in subsection (1A), including a function or power delegated to the Clerk under—
    (a) this Act; or
    (b) any other Act; or
    (c) the Standing Orders of the House of Representatives.
  (1A) The following persons may be a delegate:
    (a) the Deputy Clerk, or other officer, of the House of Representatives:
    (b) the Chief Executive of the Parliamentary Service:
    (c) an employee of the Parliamentary Service:
    (d) a person working for the Clerk of the House of Representatives or the Parliamentary Service as a contractor or secondee.
  (1B) A delegation must be made in writing.

## Part 2 — Amendment to Parliamentary Service Act 2000

### 5 Amendment to Parliamentary Service Act 2000
This Part amends the Parliamentary Service Act 2000.

### 6 Schedule 1 amended
In Schedule 1, replace clause 8(1) with:
  (1) The Chief Executive may delegate any function, duty, or power the Chief Executive may exercise under an enactment or under a delegation made under an enactment, other than this power of delegation, to—
    (a) a specified employee, or a specified class of employees, of the Parliamentary Service; or
    (b) the Clerk, Deputy Clerk, another specified officer, or a specified class of officers, of the House of Representatives; or
    (c) a person working for the Parliamentary Service or the Clerk of the House of Representatives as a contractor or secondee.
