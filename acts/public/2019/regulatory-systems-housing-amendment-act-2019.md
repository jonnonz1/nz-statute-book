# Regulatory Systems (Housing) Amendment Act 2019
_Consolidation as at 2019-11-13_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Regulatory Systems (Housing) Amendment Act 2019.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force immediately after the expiry of the 2-month period that starts on the date of Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Amendments to Public and Community Housing Management Act 1992

### 3 Amendments to Public and Community Housing Management Act 1992
This Part amends the Public and Community Housing Management Act 1992.

### 4 Section 172 amended (Register of community housing providers)
(1) In section 172(c), replace the full details with in the case of a registered provider who obtains a class of registration that qualifies that provider as being eligible to receive Crown grants or assets of the Crown or a Crown entity, the full details.
(2) In section 172, insert as subsection (2):
  (2) Subsection (1)﻿(b) does not require the authority to include on the register information about a person whose membership on a provider’s governing body ended before the provider became a registered community housing provider, but the authority has discretion to include that information.

## Part 2 — Amendments to Retirement Villages Act 2003

### 5 Amendments to Retirement Villages Act 2003
This Part amends the Retirement Villages Act 2003.

### 6 New section 3A inserted (Transitional, savings, and related provisions)

### 7 Section 79 amended (Contraventions of certain provisions an offence)
