# Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1974
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1974, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956.
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 18th day of April 1974.

### 2
[Repealed]

### 3
This section repealed para (e) and added paras (f) and (g) to section 8 of the Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1969.

### 4 Application of this Act
The adjustments to any retiring allowance, annual allowance, or annuity payable immediately before the commencement of this Act shall be made as if this Act had commenced on the 31st day of March 1956:
  Provided that no increased payment shall be made in respect of any period before the commencement of this Act.
