# Maori Purposes Act 1974
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the law relating to Maoris and Maori land, and for other purposes

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Maori Purposes Act 1974.

## Part 1 — Amendments to Maori Affairs Act 1953

### 2 This Part to form part of the Maori Affairs Act 1953
This Part of this Act shall be read together with and deemed part of the Maori Affairs Act 1953 (in this Part referred to as the principal Act).

### 3 Amendment of names of land owners in Court records
The principal Act is hereby amended by inserting, after section 60, the following section:
  On the application of an owner of Maori freehold land the Registrar may amend any entry in the records of the Court relating to the interest of the applicant in the land if he is satisfied that an amendment is necessary to show correctly the name of the applicant, or the name by which the applicant is or desires to be commonly known, including, in the case of an applicant who is a married woman, her married name.

## Part 2 — Amendments to Maori Community Development Act 1962

### 6 This Part to form part of the Maori Community Development Act 1962
This Part of this Act shall be read together with and deemed part of the Maori [Community Development] Act 1962 (in this Part referred to as the principal Act).

### 7 Definition of Maori
Section 2 of the principal Act is hereby amended by omitting the definition of the term Maori, and substituting the following definition:.
  - **Maori** means a person of the Maori race of New Zealand; and includes any descendant of such a person

### 8 Welfare officers to be known as Community Officers
(1) The principal Act is hereby amended by omitting from section 4, subsection (1) of section 5, and subsections (1) and (2) of section 6 the words Welfare officers wherever they occur, and substituting in each case the words Community Officers.
(2) The principal Act is hereby further amended by omitting from subsection (4) of section 5, and paragraph (d) of subsection (2) of section 34 the words Welfare Officer wherever they occur, and substituting in each case the words Community Officer.
(3) Every person who, at the commencement of this Act, is holding office as a Welfare Officer under section 4 of the principal Act shall be deemed to have been duly appointed under the State Services Act 1962 as a Community Officer.
(4) Every person who, at the commencement of this Act, is holding office as an honorary Welfare Officer under section 5 of the principal Act shall be deemed to have been duly appointed under that section as an honorary Community Officer.

## Part 3 — Miscellaneous Amendments

### 9 Extension of hall site for Ngati Poneke Maori Association
(1) The land described in subsection (4) of this section (in this section hereinafter referred to as the land) is hereby vested in the Maori Trustee for an estate of freehold in fee simple for the purpose of a lease to the Ngati Poneke Maori Association as an addition to the area leased to the Association for a hall site by a lease (in this section hereinafter referred to as the first lease) granted pursuant to section 15 of the Maori Purposes Act 1969.
(2) The Maori Trustee may lease the land to the Ngati Poneke Maori Association upon the same terms and conditions as those set out in the first lease, and may grant a separate lease of which the term shall terminate on the same date as the term of the first lease, or grant a lease covering both parcels of land in substitution for the first lease.
(3) The provisions of subsection (4A) of section 15 of the Maori Purposes Act 1969 (as inserted by section 12 of the Maori Purposes Act 1970) shall apply to the land.
(4) The land to which this section relates is particularly described as follows:First, all that parcel of land situated in the Wellington Land District, containing 1,069 square metres, more or less, situated in Block VII, Port Nicholson Survey District, being Section 1269, Town of Wellington (City of Wellington), and being the whole of the land comprised and described in certificate of title, Volume 7C, folio 1250, Wellington Registry (SO Plan 27831).Secondly, all that parcel of land in the Wellington Land District, containing 376 square metres, more or less, situated in Block VII, Port Nicholson Survey District, being Subdivision A of Section 542 and Subdivision B of Section 544, Town of Wellington (City of Wellington), and being part of the land comprised and described in Proclamation 3211 (SO Plan 20866).Thirdly, all that parcel of land in the Wellington Land District, containing 1,456 square metres, more or less, situated in Block VII, Port Nicholson Survey District, being part of Section 544, Town of Wellington (City of Wellington), and being part of the land comprised and described in Gazette Notice 610240.Fourthly, all that parcel of land in the Wellington Land District containing 375 square metres, more or less, situated in Block VII, Port Nicholson Survey District, being part of Section 544, Town of Wellington (City of Wellington), and being the whole of the land comprised and described in Proclamation 3799 (SO Plan 21511).

### 10 Leases of dwellings under the Maori Housing Amendment Act 1938
Section 13 of the Maori Housing Amendment Act 1938 is hereby amended by omitting the words on a monthly or weekly tenancy or for any term not exceeding three years at any one time.

### 11 Right of way around Lake Taupo and on banks of rivers or streams flowing into lake
(1) Section 14 of the Maori Land Amendment and Maori Land Claims Adjustment Act 1926 is hereby amended by inserting, after subsection (3), the following subsection:
  (3A) For the purposes of subsection (3) of this section, the margin of the said lake shall be the 358.378 metres contour in terms of Taupo Datum (which is the same as 357.015 metres in terms of Moturiki Datum), whether or not that contour is for the time being covered by the waters of the said lake, or such other margin as may from time to time by defined by the Governor-General by proclamation.
(2) The said section 14 is hereby further amended by inserting, after subsection (4), the following subsection:
  (4A) The Governor-General may from time to time, by proclamation, define any area of land over which a right of way for the time being exists under subsection (3) or subsection (4)(b) of this section, being land which on the 26th day of September 1926 was Maori customary land or Maori freehold land. Every Proclamation under this subsection shall have effect according to its tenor.
(3) The said section 14 is hereby further amended—
  (a) By omitting from subsection (3) the words one chain, and substituting the words 20 metres:
  (b) By omitting from subsection (4)(b) the words one chain, and substituting the words 20 metres.
