# Insurance Companies' Deposits Amendment Act 1974
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Insurance Companies' Deposits Act 1953

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Insurance Companies' Deposits Amendment Act 1974, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Insurance Companies' Deposits Act 1953 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) Sections 3 and 5 of this Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 26th day of August 1974.
(3) Except as provided in subsection (2) of this section, this Act shall come into force on the date of its passing.

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection substituted the definition of approved securities in section 2 of the principal Act.
