# Local Government Amendment Act (No 3) 1991
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Local Government Act 1974

### 1 Short Title
This Act may by cited as the Local Government Amendment Act (No 3) 1991, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Local Government Act 1974 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Road access
(1) This subsection amended 321(3)(c) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 321(3)(e)(i) of the principal Act.
(3) This subsection inserted s 321(3)(ca) of the principal Act.

### 3 Validation
Any action taken or decision made under the Local Government Act 1974 or the Resource Management Act 1991 in the period commencing on the 1st day of October 1991 and ending with the commencement of this Act is hereby declared to be, and to have always been, as valid as it would have been if this Act had been in force when that action was taken or that decision was made.
