# Rehabilitation Act Repeal Act 1991
_Consolidation as at 1991-08-01_

> An Act to repeal the Rehabilitation Act 1941 and to make provision incidental thereto

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Rehabilitation Act Repeal Act 1991.

### 2 Repeal of Rehabilitation Act 1941, etc
(1) The enactments specified in Part 1 of the Schedule are hereby repealed.
(2) The regulations and orders specified in Part 2 of the Schedule are hereby revoked.

### 3 Savings
If, immediately before the commencement of this section, a person was in receipt of assistance under any of the Acts, regulations, or orders repealed or revoked by section 2, that person shall continue to be entitled to receive that assistance as if those Acts or those regulations or orders had not been repealed or revoked for such period as the Secretary for War Pensions considers that the assistance is required.
