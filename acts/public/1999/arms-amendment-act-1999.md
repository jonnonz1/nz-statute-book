# Arms Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Arms Act 1983

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Arms Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Arms Act 1983 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 2 New heading and sections inserted
This section inserted s 65A to 65H and the preceding heading of the principal Act.
