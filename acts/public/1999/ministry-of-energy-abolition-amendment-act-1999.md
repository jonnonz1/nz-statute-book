# Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend The Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Amendment Act 1999, and is part of The Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Regulations
This section inserted Section 35(2), (3), (4), and (5) into the principal Act.
