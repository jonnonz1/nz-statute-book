# Immigration Amendment Act (No 2) 1999
_Consolidation as at 2010-11-29_

> An Act to bring forward the date of commencement of certain provisions of the Immigration Amendment Act 1999

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Immigration Amendment Act (No 2) 1999, and is part of the Immigration Act 1987.

### 2 Commencement of Immigration Amendment Act 1999
This section inserted s 1(2A) of the Immigration Amendment Act 1999.
