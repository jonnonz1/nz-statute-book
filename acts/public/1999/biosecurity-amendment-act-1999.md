# Biosecurity Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Biosecurity Act 1993

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Biosecurity Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Biosecurity Act 1993 (the principal Act).

### 2 Other powers of Ministers
This section amended s 11(2) of the principal Act.

### 3 Penalties
This section amended s 157(7) of the principal Act.

### 4 New sections substituted
This section inserted s 159A, substituted s 160, and repealed s 160A of the principal Act.

### 5 Amendments to Biosecurity Amendment Act 1997
(1) This subsection amended s 121(1) Biosecurity Amendment Act 1997.
(2) This subsection amended s 122 Biosecurity Amendment Act 1997.

### 6 Consequential amendment to Summary Proceedings Act 1957
This section substituted paragraph (e) of the definition of the term infringement notice in s 2 of the Summary Proceedings Act 1957.
