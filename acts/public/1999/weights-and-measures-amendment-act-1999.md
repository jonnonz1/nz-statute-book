# Weights and Measures Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Weights and Measures Act 1987

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Weights and Measures Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Weights and Measures Act 1987 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the 28th day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 2 Interpretation
This section substituted the definition of infringement offence in section 2(1) of the principal Act.

### 3 Regulations
This section inserted section 41(ya) of the principal Act.

### 4 Infringement fee
This section substituted section 33A of the principal Act.

### 5 Schedule 1 amended
This section amended Schedule 1 of the principal Act.
