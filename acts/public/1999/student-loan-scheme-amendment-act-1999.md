# Student Loan Scheme Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2012-04-01_

> An Act to amend the Student Loan Scheme Act 1992

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Student Loan Scheme Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Student Loan Scheme Act 1992 (the principal Act).

### 2 Payment of terminal repayment obligation
(1) This subsection substituted section 30(1) of the principal Act.
(2) Subsection (1) applies to terminal repayment obligations payable for the 1997-98 and subsequent income years.

### 3 Periods in which interest write-off and interest reduction apply
(1) This subsection amended Section 41(c) of the principal Act.
(2) Subsection (1) applies on and after 1 April 1999.

### 4 Excess repayments made by residents
(1) This subsection inserted Section 56(2B) of the principal Act.
(2) Section 5 of the Student Loan Scheme Amendment Act (No 2) 1998 is repealed.
(3) Subsection (1) applies to amounts paid in excess of borrowers' repayment obligations that arise on and after 1 April 2000.
(4) Subsection (2) applies on and after 7 October 1998.
