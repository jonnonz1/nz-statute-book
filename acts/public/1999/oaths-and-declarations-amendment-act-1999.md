# Oaths and Declarations Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Oaths and Declarations Act 1957

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Oaths and Declarations Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Oaths and Declarations Act 1957 (the principal Act).

### 2 Persons required to take Oath of Allegiance and Judicial Oath
This section amended Schedule 2 of the principal Act.
