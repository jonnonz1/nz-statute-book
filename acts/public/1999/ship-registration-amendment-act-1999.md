# Ship Registration Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Ship Registration Act 1992

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Ship Registration Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Ship Registration Act 1992 (the principal Act).

### 2 Flags authorised under prerogative
This section inserted section 58A into the principal Act.
