# Insolvency Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-12-03_

> An Act to amend the Insolvency Act 1967

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Insolvency Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Insolvency Act 1967 (the principal Act).

### 2 New sections substituted
This section substituted section 28, and inserted s 28A of the principal Act.

### 3 Assignee to advertise adjudication
This section amended s 31 of the principal Act.

### 4 New sections inserted
This section inserted sections 93A to 93I of the principal Act.
