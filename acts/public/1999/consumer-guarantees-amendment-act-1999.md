# Consumer Guarantees Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Consumer Guarantees Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 2 Jurisdiction
(1) This section amended s 47 of the principal Act.
(2) This section amended s 47(6) of the principal Act.
