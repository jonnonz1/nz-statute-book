# Animal Identification Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Animal Identification Act 1993

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Animal Identification Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Animal Identification Act 1993 (the principal Act).

### 2 Recovery of stray stock
This section amended s 9(7) of the principal Act.
