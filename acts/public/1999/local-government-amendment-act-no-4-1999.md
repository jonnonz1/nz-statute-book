# Local Government Amendment Act (No 4) 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Local Government Act 1974

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government Amendment Act (No 4) 1999, and is part of the Local Government Act 1974 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.
