# Conservation Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Conservation Act 1987

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Conservation Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Conservation Act 1987 (the principal Act).

### 2 Membership
This section substituted s 6D(2)(d) of the principal Act.

### 3 Term of office of members of Authority
This section inserted s 6F(4) to (4B) of the principal Act.

### 4 Membership of Conservation Boards
This section substituted s 6P(4)(d) of the principal Act.

### 5 Term of office of members of Boards
This section substituted s 6R(4), and inserted s 6R(4A) and (4B) of the principal Act.
