# Summary Offences Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Summary Offences Act 1981

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Summary Offences Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Summary Offences Act 1981 (the principal Act).

### 2 Publishing document or thing explaining manufacture of explosives, etc
(1) This subsection amended Section 8(1) of the principal Act.
(2) Section 2(1) of the principal Act is amended by repealing the definition of the term unlawful weapon.
