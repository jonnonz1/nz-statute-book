# New Zealand Film Commission Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the New Zealand Film Commission Act 1978

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the New Zealand Film Commission Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the New Zealand Film Commission Act 1978 (the principal Act).

### 2 Estimates of expenditure
Section 21 of the principal Act is repealed
