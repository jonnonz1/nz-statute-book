# Marriage Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Marriage Act 1955

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Marriage Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Marriage Act 1955 (the principal Act).

### 2 Issue of marriage licence and information return
This section inserted Section 24(1A) of the principal Act.

### 3 Place and form of marriage before marriage celebrant
This section amended Section 31(2) of the principal Act.

### 4 Marriages before Registrar
(1) This section amended Section 33(1) of the principal Act.

### 5 Repeal of spent enactment
The Marriage Amendment Act 1994 is repealed.
