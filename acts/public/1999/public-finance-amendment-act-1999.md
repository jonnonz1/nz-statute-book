# Public Finance Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Public Finance Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Public Finance Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Public Finance Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
