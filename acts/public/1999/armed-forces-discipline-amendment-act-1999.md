# Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
This section substituted para (d) and repealed para (h) of the definition of the term commanding officer in s 2(1) of the principal Act

### 3 Compensation for loss of or damage to property
This section amended s 86(1) of the principal Act.
