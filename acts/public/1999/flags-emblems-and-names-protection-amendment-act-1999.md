# Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Act 1981

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Act 1981 (the principal Act).

### 2 Defence
This section inserted s 20AA of the principal Act.

### 3 Injunction for breach of section 20A
This section amended s 20B(1) of the principal Act.
