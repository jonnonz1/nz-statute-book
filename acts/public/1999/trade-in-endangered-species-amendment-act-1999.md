# Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Scientific authorities
This section substituted Section 7(1)(c), (d), and (e) of the principal Act.
