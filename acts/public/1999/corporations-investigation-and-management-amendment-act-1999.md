# Corporations (Investigation and Management) Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Corporations (Investigation and Management) Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Corporations (Investigation and Management) Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Corporations (Investigation and Management) Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Statutory management of corporations and associated persons
This section inserted s 38(3) and (4) of the principal Act.

### 3 Moratorium
(1) This subsection amended s 42(5) and (6) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 42(7) of the principal Act.

### 4 Statutory manager may suspend payment of money owing
(1) This subsection amended s 44(3) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 44(4) of the principal Act.

### 5 Management of corporation to vest in statutory manager
This section amended s 45(1) of the principal Act.

### 6 Application of certain provisions of Companies Act 1993
(1) This subsection amended s 55(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection substituted s 55(1)(c) of the principal Act.

### 7 Termination of statutory management
  (a) This paragraph amended section 62(1) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended section 62(3)(a) to (c) of the principal Act.
  (c) This paragraph amended section 62(4) of the principal Act.
  (d) This paragraph amended section 62(4)(a) and (b) of the principal Act.
  (e) This paragraph amended section 62(5)(a) of the principal Act.
  (f) This paragraph amended section 62(5)(b) of the principal Act.
