# Law Practitioners Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2008-08-01_

> An Act to amend the Law Practitioners Act 1982

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Law Practitioners Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Law Practitioners Act 1982 (the principal Act).

### 2 Council may make rules
(1) This subsection inserted Section 17(2)(ea) into the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted Section 17(2)(fa) into the principal Act.
