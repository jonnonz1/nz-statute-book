# State Sector Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the State Sector Act 1988

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the State Sector Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the State Sector Act 1988 (the principal Act).

### 2 Schedule 1 amended
This section amended Schedule 1 of the principal Act.
