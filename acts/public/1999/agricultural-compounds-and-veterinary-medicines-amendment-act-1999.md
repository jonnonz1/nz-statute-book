# Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Amendment Act 1999
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Act 1997

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Act 1997 (the principal Act).

### 2 Register of agricultural compounds
This section amended s 24(6) of the principal Act.
