# Taxation (Budget Measures: Family Incomes Package) Act 2017
_Consolidation as at 2017-12-20_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Taxation (Budget Measures: Family Incomes Package) Act 2017.

### 2 Commencement
(1) This Act comes into force on the date on which it receives the Royal assent, except as provided in this section.
(2) Part 1 comes into force on 1 April 2018.
(3) Part 2 other than section 24 comes into force on 1 April 2018.
(4) Section 24 is treated as coming into force on 30 May 2016.
