# Companies (Registration of Charges) Amendment Act 2001
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Companies (Registration of Charges) Amendment Act 2001.
(2) In this Act, the Companies (Registration of Charges) Act 1993 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Savings
Section 6(1) of the principal Act is amended by adding the words , and may be amended as if they had not been repealed.
