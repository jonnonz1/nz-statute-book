# Criminal Justice Amendment Act 2001
_Consolidation as at 2013-07-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Criminal Justice Amendment Act 2001.
(2) In this Act, the Criminal Justice Act 1985 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Determination of application for release to home detention
Section 103B of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after subsection (6), the following subsection:
  (6A) The Board may revoke a direction that an offender be released to serve his or her sentence by way of home detention, at any time before the offender is released.
