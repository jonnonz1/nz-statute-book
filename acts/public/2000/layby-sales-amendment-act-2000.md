# Layby Sales Amendment Act 2000
_Consolidation as at 2014-06-17_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Layby Sales Amendment Act 2000.
(2) In this Act, The Layby Sales Act 1971 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Act not to apply to certain layby sales
Section 4(a) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression $1,000, and substituting the expression $7,500.
