# Fair Trading Amendment Act 2000
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Fair Trading Amendment Act 2000.
(2) In this Act, the Fair Trading Act 1986 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 False representations
Section 13 of the principal Act is amended by—
  (a) omitting the words Falsely represent in each place where they occur, and substituting in each case the words make a false or misleading representation; and
  (b) inserting in the heading, after the word False, the words or misleading.
