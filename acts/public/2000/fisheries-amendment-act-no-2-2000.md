# Fisheries Amendment Act (No 2) 2000
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Fisheries Amendment Act (No 2) 2000.
(2) In this Act, the Fisheries Act 1983 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Certificates and official documents
Section 106(1)(d) of the principal Act is amended by omitting from both subparagraph (i) and subparagraph (ii) the words Royal New Zealand Naval Hydrographer, and substituting in each case the words chief executive of Land Information New Zealand.
