# Driftnet Prohibition Amendment Act 2000
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Driftnet Prohibition Amendment Act 2000.
(2) In this Act, the Driftnet Prohibition Act 1991 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Evidence in proceedings
Section 29(1)(d) of the principal Act is amended by omitting from both subparagraph (i) and subparagraph (ii) the words Royal New Zealand Naval Hydrographer, and substituting in each case the words chief executive of Land Information New Zealand.
