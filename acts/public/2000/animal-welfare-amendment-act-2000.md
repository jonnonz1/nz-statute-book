# Animal Welfare Amendment Act 2000
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Animal Welfare Amendment Act 2000.
(2) In this Act, the Animal Welfare Act 1999 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 2(1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting in paragraph (a) of the definition of the term trap, after the word mechanical, the words or adhesive.
