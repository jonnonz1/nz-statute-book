# Gambling Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-04_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Gambling Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Gambling Act 2003 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 286 amended (Bank accounts)
(1) In section 286(2), delete only by cheque signed.
(2) After section 286(2), insert:
  (3) Cash may not be withdrawn from an account referred to in subsections (1) and (2).
