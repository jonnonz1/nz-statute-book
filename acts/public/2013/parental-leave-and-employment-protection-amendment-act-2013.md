# Parental Leave and Employment Protection Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-04_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Parental Leave and Employment Protection Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Parental Leave and Employment Protection Act 1987 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 71C amended (Interpretation of this Part)
In section 71C, definition of self-employed person's average weekly earnings, paragraph (b), replace section 71CB(1A) with section 71CB(2).
