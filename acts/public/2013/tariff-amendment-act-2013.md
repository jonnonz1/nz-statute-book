# Tariff Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-04_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Tariff Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Tariff Act 1988 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 9C amended (Application of Legislation Act 2012)
In section 9C (as substituted by section 77(3) of the Legislation Act 2012), replace 1 July 2011 with the date on which section 77(3) of the Legislation Act 2012 comes into force.
