# Local Government Act 2002 Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-04_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Local Government Act 2002 Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Local Government Act 2002 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 259J repealed (Application of Regulations (Disallowance) Act 1989 to standards incorporated by reference)
Repeal section 259J.
