# Wildlife Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-04-19_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Wildlife Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the earlier of the following:
  (a) the date appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council:
  (b) the date that is 2 years after the date on which the Act receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Wildlife Act 1953 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 9 amended (Wildlife sanctuaries)
(1) In section 9(1), replace by Proclamation with by Order in Council made on the recommendation of the Minister.
(2) In section 9(1), replace no Proclamation with no Order in each place.
(3) In section 9(1) and (4), replace such Proclamation with such Order.
(4) In section 9(1)(aa), delete within the meaning of the Harbours Act 1950.
(5) In section 9(2), replace by Proclamation with by Order in Council.
(6) In section 9(3) and (4), replace a Proclamation with an Order in Council.
(7) In section 9, replace the Proclamation with the Order in each place.

### 5 Section 10 amended (All wildlife in sanctuaries absolutely protected)
In section 10, replace Proclamation with Order in Council.

### 6 Section 64 amended (Offences in relation to wildlife sanctuaries)
In section 64, replace Proclamation with Order in Council.

### 7 Section 67F amended (Penalties for other offences)
In section 67F(4)(d), replace a Proclamation with an Order in Council.
