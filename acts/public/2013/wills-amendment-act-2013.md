# Wills Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-04_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Wills Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Wills Act 2007 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 40A amended (Application of Wills (Validity) Amendment Act 2012)
In the heading to section 40A, delete (Validity).
