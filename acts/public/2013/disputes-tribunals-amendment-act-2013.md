# Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 2013
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-04_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 2013.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Disputes Tribunals Act 1988 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 7 amended (Appointment of Referees)
In section 7(3), replace term of 3 years with term not exceeding 5 years.
