# Phosphate Commission of New Zealand Dissolution Act 1989
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

> An Act to dissolve the Phosphate Commission of New Zealand

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Phosphate Commission of New Zealand Dissolution Act 1989.

### 2 Dissolving Phosphate Commission of New Zealand
(1) The Phosphate Commission of New Zealand is hereby dissolved; and all rights, assets, liabilities, and debts that the Commission had immediately before the commencement of this Act shall, on that commencement, become rights, assets, liabilities, and debts of the Minister of Agriculture.
(2) The following enactments are hereby repealed:
  (a) the Phosphate Commission of New Zealand Act 1981:
  (b) the Phosphate Commission of New Zealand Amendment Act 1982:
  (c) the Phosphate Commission of New Zealand Amendment Act 1986.
(3), (4) Amendment(s) incorporated in the Act(s).
