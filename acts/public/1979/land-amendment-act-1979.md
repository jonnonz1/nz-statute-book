# Land Amendment Act 1979
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Land Act 1948

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Land Amendment Act 1979, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Land Act 1948 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Classification of Crown land
This section substituted section 51(1)(d) of the principal Act.

### 5 Consequential amendments
(1) This subsection inserted definitions of pastoral land, pastoral lease, and pastoral occupation licence in section 2 of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended section 85(1) of the principal Act.

### 6 Repeal
Section 3 of the Land Amendment Act 1970 is hereby consequentially repealed.
