# Coroners (Coronial Cap) Amendment Act 2022
_Consolidation as at 2022-05-24_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Coroners (Coronial Cap) Amendment Act 2022.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date of Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Coroners Act 2006.

### 4 Section 109 amended (Maximum number of coroners)
Substantive amendment
(1) In section 109(1), replace 20 with 22.
Consequential amendment
(2) In section 109(2)﻿(e), replace 13.5 with 21.5.
