# Secondary Legislation Confirmation Act 2022
_Consolidation as at 2022-12-16_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Secondary Legislation Confirmation Act 2022.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Preliminary provisions

### 3 Purpose
The purpose of this Act is to confirm—
  (a) instruments that, unless earlier confirmed by an Act of Parliament, are—
    (i) revoked at the applicable deadline under section 47C(1)﻿(a) or (b) of the Legislation Act 2012; or
    (ii) taken after the applicable deadline to have been invalid for their past operation (because they have been revoked with effect before or on the deadline) under section 47D of the Legislation Act 2012; and
  (b) instruments that, unless earlier confirmed by an Act of Parliament, are—
    (i) revoked at the applicable deadline under section 123 of the Legislation Act 2019; or
    (ii) taken after the applicable deadline to have been invalid for their past operation (because they have been revoked with effect before or on the deadline) under section 126 of the Legislation Act 2019.

### 4 Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—
  - **deadline** ,—
    (a) in relation to confirmation under the Legislation Act 2012, has the meaning given in section 4 of that Act; and
    (b) in relation to confirmation under the Legislation Act 2019, has the meaning given in section 121 of that Act
  - **instrument** means,—
    (a) in relation to confirmation under the Legislation Act 2012, a confirmable instrument or an annual confirmable instrument as defined in section 47B of that Act; and
    (b) in relation to confirmation under the Legislation Act 2019, secondary legislation that must be confirmed under subpart 3 of Part 5 of that Act.

### 5 Act binds the Crown
This Act binds the Crown.

### 6 Repeal
The Subordinate Legislation Confirmation Act 2021 (2021 No 56) is repealed.
