# Subordinate Legislation Confirmation Act 2021
_Consolidation as at 2021-12-13_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Subordinate Legislation Confirmation Act 2021.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Preliminary provisions

### 3 Purpose
The purpose of this Act is to confirm certain confirmable instruments and annual confirmable instruments that, unless earlier confirmed by an Act of Parliament, are—
  (a) revoked at the applicable deadline under section 47C(1)﻿(a) or (b) of the Legislation Act 2012; or
  (b) taken after the applicable deadline to have been invalid for their past operation (because they have been revoked with effect before or on the deadline) under section 47D of the Legislation Act 2012.

### 4 Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, confirmable instrument and annual confirmable instrument have the same meanings as in section 47B of the Legislation Act 2012.

### 5 Act binds the Crown
This Act binds the Crown.

### 6 Repeal
The Subordinate Legislation Confirmation Act 2020 (2020 No 66) is repealed.
