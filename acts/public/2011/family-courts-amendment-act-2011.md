# Family Courts Amendment Act 2011
_Consolidation as at 2011-07-22_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Family Courts Amendment Act 2011.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Family Courts Act 1980.

### 4 Application of District Courts Act 1947
Section 16(4) is amended by omitting 79 and substituting 78A.
