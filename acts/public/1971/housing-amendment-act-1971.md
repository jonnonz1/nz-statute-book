# Housing Amendment Act 1971
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Housing Act 1955

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Housing Amendment Act 1971, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Housing Act 1955 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
