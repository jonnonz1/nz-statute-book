# Rent Restriction Act 1924
_Consolidation as at 2017-06-03_

> An Act to amend Part 1 of the War Legislation Amendment Act 1916 (relating to the restriction of rent), and to continue its operation.

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Rent Restriction Act 1924, and shall be read together with and deemed part of Part 1 of the War Legislation Amendment Act 1916.

### 2 Further extension of duration of law relating to restriction of rent
[Repealed]

### 3 Covenant to exclude application to any dwellinghouse of Part 1 of War Legislation Amendment Act 1916, to be void
[Repealed]

### 4 At option of landlord, standard rent for a period of 1 year may be 8 per centum of capital value as on 3 August, 1914. Consequential repeal.
[Repealed]

### 5 Where rent payable on 3 August, 1914, cannot be ascertained, standard rent may be fixed by reference to capital value
[Repealed]

### 6 Obtaining possession of dwellinghouse by misrepresentation
Section 10 of the Housing Amendment Act 1921–22, is hereby amended as follows:
  (a) by inserting, after the words section 9 hereof in subsection (1), the words or who obtains possession of a dwellinghouse by representation that he requires possession on that ground; and by omitting from the same subsection the words by means of such order:
  (b) by omitting from subsection (3) the words upon the said ground (e), and substituting the words upon the ground defined in paragraph (e) of subsection (1) of section 9 hereof, or where possession has been obtained by representation that possession is required on that ground; and by omitting from the same subsection the words by means of such order:
  (c) by omitting from subsection (4) the words the making of the order for possession, and substituting the words possession was obtained.
