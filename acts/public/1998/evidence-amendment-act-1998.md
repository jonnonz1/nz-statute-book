# Evidence Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Evidence Act 1908

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Evidence Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Evidence Act 1908 (the principal Act).

### 2 Copy of Act of Parliament, Imperial legislation, and regulations printed as prescribed to be evidence
This section amended s 29 of the principal Act.

### 3 Copy of reprint of Act, Imperial legislation, or regulations to be evidence
This section substituted s 29A of the principal Act.
