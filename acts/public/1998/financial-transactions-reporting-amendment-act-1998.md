# Financial Transactions Reporting Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 1996

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Financial Transactions Reporting Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 1996 (the principal Act).

### 2 Financial institutions to report suspicious transactions
(1) This subsection inserted s 15(2)(ca) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 15(3) of the principal Act.

### 3 Protection of persons reporting suspicious transactions
(1) This subsection substituted s 17(1), and inserted s 17(A) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 17(3) of the principal ˙Act.

### 4 Protection of identity of persons making suspicious transaction reports
(1) This subsection inserted s 21(1)(c) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 21(3) of the principal Act.

### 5 Offences
(1) This subsection amended s 22(3) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 22(8) of the principal Act.

### 6 Application of Privacy Act 1993
This section amended s 28 of the principal Act.

### 7 Obligation to keep transaction records
This section inserted s 29(2)(f) of the principal Act.

### 8 Consultation not required for consolidation of certain regulations
This section inserted s 56A of the principal Act.

### 9 New Schedule substituted
This section substituted the Schedule of the principal Act.
