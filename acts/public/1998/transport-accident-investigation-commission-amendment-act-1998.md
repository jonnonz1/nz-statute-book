# Transport Accident Investigation Commission Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Transport Accident Investigation Commission Act 1990

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Transport Accident Investigation Commission Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Transport Accident Investigation Commission Act 1990 (the principal Act).

### 2 Renumbering of paragraph
This section renumbered s 12(1)(d) of the principal Act as s 12(1)(ca).

### 3 Offences in respect of certain powers of investigation
(1) This subsection amended s 12A(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 12A(2)(b) of the principal Act.
