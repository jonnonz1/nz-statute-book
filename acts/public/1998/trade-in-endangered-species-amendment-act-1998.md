# Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
This section inserted s 3(5) of the principal Act.

### 3 Application for permit or certificate
This section inserted s 10(5A) of the principal Act.

### 4 Permit to import endangered species
This section amended s 14(d) of the principal Act.

### 5 Permit to import threatened species
This section substituted s 18 of the principal Act.

### 6 Permit to import exploited species
This section substituted s 22 of the principal Act.

### 7 Endangered species as personal or household effects
This section amended s 30(2) of the principal Act.

### 8 Certificate for specimens bred in captivity or artificially propagated
This section amended s 31(2) of the principal Act.

### 9 Rights of entry
  (a) This paragraph inserted s 37(1)(d) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended s 37(2) of the principal Act.
  (c) This paragraph inserted s 37(7) of the principal Act.

### 10 Powers of search
This section inserted s 38(3) of the principal Act.

### 11 Taking and analysis of samples of specimens
This section inserted s 43A of the principal Act.

### 12 Possession of specimens of endangered, threatened, or exploited species
This section amended s 45 of the principal Act.

### 13 Regulations
This section inserted s 54(ea) of the principal Act.
