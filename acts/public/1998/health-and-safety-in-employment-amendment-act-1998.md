# Health and Safety in Employment Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2016-04-04_

> An Act to amend the Health and Safety in Employment Act 1992

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Health and Safety in Employment Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Health and Safety in Employment Act 1992 (the principal Act).

### 2 Duties of persons who control places of work
This section substituted s 16 of the principal Act.

### 3 Codes of practice
(1) This subsection inserted s 20(1)﻿(aa) and (ab) of the principal Act.
(2)
  (a) This paragraph amended the definition of the term approved code of practice in section 2(1) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended s 20(2) of the principal Act.

### 4 Other offences
(1) This subsection amended s 50(1)﻿(a) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 50(2) of the principal Act.
