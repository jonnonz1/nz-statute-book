# Privacy Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend The Privacy Act 1993

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Privacy Amendment Act 1998, and is part of The Privacy Act 1993 (the principal Act).

### 2 Schedule 5 amended
This section amended the part of Schedule 5 of the principal Act headed Police Records.

### 3 Repeal
Section 9 of The Privacy Amendment Act 1996 is repealed.
