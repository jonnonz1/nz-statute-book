# Investment Advisers (Disclosure) Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Investment Advisers (Disclosure) Act 1996

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Investment Advisers (Disclosure) Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Investment Advisers (Disclosure) Act 1996 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection substituted paragraph (b) of the definitions of the terms investment broker and broker in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection substituted the definitions of the terms investment money and money in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(3) This subsection substituted the definitions of the terms investment property and property in s 2(1) of the principal Act.

### 3 Initial disclosure by investment advisers and investment brokers
(1) This subsection amended s 3(2) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 3(3) of the principal Act.
