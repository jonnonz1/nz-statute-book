# Forestry Rights Registration Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Forestry Rights Registration Act 1983

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Forestry Rights Registration Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Forestry Rights Registration Act 1983 (the principal Act).

### 2 Definitions
(1) This subsection omitted the definitions of the terms forestry right, grantor, and grantee from s 2 of the principal.
(2) This subsection inserted the definitions of the terms Forestry right, Holder and Proprietor in s 2 of the principal Act.

### 3 Creation of forestry rights
This section inserted s 2A of the principal Act.

### 4 Forestry rights to be profit a prendre
  (a) This paragraph amended s 3(2A) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended s 3(2) of the principal Act.

### 5 Plan requirements
  (a) This paragraph amended s 5(2) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended s 5(2) of the principal Act.
  (c) This paragraph amended s 5(2) of the principal Act.
  (d) This paragraph amended s 5(3) of the principal Act.

### 6 Registration of forestry rights over unregistered land
  (a) This paragraph amended s 5A(1) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended s 5A(1) of the principal Act.
  (c) This paragraph amended s 5A(2) of the principal Act.
  (d) This paragraph amended s 5A(2) of the principal Act.
  (e) This paragraph amended s 5A(3) of the principal Act.
  (f) This paragraph amended s 5A(3) of the principal Act.

### 7 Subdivision and rights of way
This section amended s 6 of the principal Act.
