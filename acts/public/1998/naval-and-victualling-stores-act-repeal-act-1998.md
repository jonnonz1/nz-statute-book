# Naval and Victualling Stores Act Repeal Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 1998-06-03_

> An Act to repeal the Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Naval and Victualling Stores Act Repeal Act 1998.

### 2 Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908 repealed
(1) The Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908 is repealed.
(2) Schedule 1 of the Defence Act 1990 is consequentially amended by repealing the item relating to section 10 of the Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908.
