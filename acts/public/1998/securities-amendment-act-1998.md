# Securities Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2014-12-01_

> An Act to amend the Securities Act 1978

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Securities Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Securities Act 1978 (the principal Act).

### 2 Amendments to Securities Amendment Act 1988
(1) Section 37(1) of the Securities Amendment Act 1988 is amended—
  (a) This paragraph repealed the definition of the term authorised dealer in foreign exchange in s 37(1) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
  (b) This paragraph amended para (b) of the definition of the term authorised futures contract in s 37(1) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
  (c) This paragraph amended para (c) of the definition of the term authorised futures contract in s 37(1) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
  (d) This paragraph amended the definition of the term registered bank in s 37(1) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
(2) This subsection amended s 37(2) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
(3) This subsection inserted s 37(10) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
(4) This subsection inserted s 38(3) Securities Amendment Act 1988.
