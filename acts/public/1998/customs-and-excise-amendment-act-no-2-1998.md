# Customs and Excise Amendment Act (No 2) 1998
_Consolidation as at 2018-10-01_

> An Act to amend the Customs and Excise Act 1996

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Customs and Excise Amendment Act (No 2) 1998, and is part of the Customs and Excise Act 1996 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection substituted the definition of the term New Zealand in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(2) Section 2(2) of the Customs and Excise Amendment Act 1996 is consequentially repealed.

### 3 Goods subject to control of Customs
(1) This subsection substituted s 20(1)﻿(a) and inserted s 20(1)﻿(aa) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 20(2) of the principal Act.

### 4 Entry of excisable goods
(1) This subsection amended s 70(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 70(1A) of the principal Act.

### 5 Regulations relating to entry of excisable goods
This section inserted s 71(aa) of the principal Act.

### 6 Other refunds and remissions of duty
This section amended s 113(3) of the principal Act.

### 7 Imposition of penalty
(1) This subsection amended s 128(7)﻿(f) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection substituted s 128(7)﻿(j) of the principal Act.
(3) This subsection substituted s 128(7)﻿(o) and (p) of the principal Act.

### 8 Giving of notice
(1) This subsection substituted s 284(1)﻿(g) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection substituted s 284(2)﻿(f) of the principal Act.
(3) This subsection substituted s 284(3)﻿(e) of the principal Act.

### 9 Transitional provisions relating to investigations of offences under Customs Act 1966
This section inserted s 305A of the principal Act.
