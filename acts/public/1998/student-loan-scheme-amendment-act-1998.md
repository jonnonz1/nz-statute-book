# Student Loan Scheme Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2012-04-01_

> An Act to amend the Student Loan Scheme Act 1992

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Student Loan Scheme Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Student Loan Scheme Act 1992 (the principal Act).

### 2 Excess repayments made by residents
(1) This subsection amended s 56(1)(f) of the principal Act.
(2) Section 56(1)(g) of the principal Act is repealed.
(3) This subsection inserted s 56(1A) and (1B) of the principal Act.
(4) This subsection amended s 56(2) of the principal Act.
(5) This subsection inserted s 56(2A) of the principal Act.
(6) Subsections (1) to (5) apply to an overpayment arising on or after 1 April 1998.

### 3 New section inserted
This section inserted section 60A of the principal Act.
