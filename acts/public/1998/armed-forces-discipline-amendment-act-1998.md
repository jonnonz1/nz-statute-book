# Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971 (the principal Act).

### 2 Regulations
  (a) This paragraph amended s 205(1)(c) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph repealed s 205(1)(c)(iii) of the principal Act.
