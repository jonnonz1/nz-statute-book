# Unit Trusts Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2014-12-01_

> An Act to amend the Unit Trusts Act 1960

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Unit Trusts Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Unit Trusts Act 1960 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection repealed the definition of the term nominated company and inserted the definitions of the terms Nominated person and Nominee in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection substituted the definition of the term trustee in s 2(1) of the principal Act.

### 3 Unit trust to have manager and trustee
This section substituted s 3(3) and (4) of the principal Act.

### 4 New sections substituted
This section substituted s 6, and inserted ss 6A to 6C of the principal Act.

### 5 Implied provisions in trust deed
  (a) This paragraph amended s 12(1)(b)(i) and (ii) of the principal Act.
  (b) This paragraph amended s 12(1)(b)(ii) of the principal Act.

### 6 Duty of manager with respect to subscriptions
This section amended s 14(2) of the principal Act.

### 7 Subscriptions, etc to become subject to trusts
This section amended s 15 of the principal Act.
