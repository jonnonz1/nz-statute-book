# Domestic Violence Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Domestic Violence Act 1995

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Domestic Violence Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Domestic Violence Act 1995 (the principal Act).

### 2 Programme providers' fees and expenses
This section substituted s 44(b) of the principal Act.

### 3 Court may appoint lawyer
This section substituted s 81(3)(b) of the principal Act.
