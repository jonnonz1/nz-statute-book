# Social Security Amendment Act (No 3) 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Social Security Act 1964

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Social Security Amendment Act (No 3) 1998, and is part of the Social Security Act 1964 (the principal Act).

### 2 Duty to supply tax file number and consequence of failure to do so
(1) This subsection substituted section 82A of the principal Act.
(2) Subsection (1) applies on and after the date on which this Act receives the Royal assent.
