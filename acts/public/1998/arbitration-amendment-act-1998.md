# Arbitration Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Arbitration Act 1996

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Arbitration Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Arbitration Act 1996 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on 1 July 1997.

### 2 Schedule 1 amended
This section amended article 7(1) of Schedule 1 of the principal Act.

### 3 Schedule 4 amended
This section amended Schedule 4 of the principal Act.
