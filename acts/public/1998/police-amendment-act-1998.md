# Police Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Police Act 1958

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Police Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Police Act 1958 (the principal Act).

### 2 General instructions
This section substituted s 30(3) and (4) of the principal Act

### 3 General search of person in custody
This section amended s 57A(1) of the principal Act.

### 4 Searchers
This section inserted s 57B of the principal Act.

### 5 Unclaimed property
(1) This section substituted s 59(2) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 59(4) of the principal Act.

### 6 Publication of Police Gazette
This section amended s 61 of the principal Act.

### 7 Consequential repeal
Section 4 of the Police Amendment Act 1981 is consequentially repealed.
