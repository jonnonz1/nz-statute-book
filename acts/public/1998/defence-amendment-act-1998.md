# Defence Amendment Act 1998
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Defence Act 1990

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Defence Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Defence Act 1990 (the principal Act).

### 2 Return of service obligation
This section amended s 53 of the principal Act.

### 3 State of critical manning
This section amended s 54(2) of the principal Act.

### 4 Notice while serving overseas, etc
This section amended s 55 of the principal Act.

### 5 Notice while subject to disciplinary proceedings, etc
This section amended s 57(2) of the principal Act.

### 6 Security of defence areas
This section amended s 93 of the principal Act is amended.

### 7 Regulations
This section inserted s 101(ga) and (gb) of the principal Act.
