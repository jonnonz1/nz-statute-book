# Mortgagors and Lessees Rehabilitation Amendment Act 1961
_Consolidation as at 2017-06-03_

> An Act to amend the Mortgagors and Lessees Rehabilitation Act 1936

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Mortgagors and Lessees Rehabilitation Amendment Act 1961, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Mortgagors and Lessees Rehabilitation Act 1936 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 High Court to exercise powers of Court of Review
The Court of Review is hereby abolished, and any jurisdiction and powers which that Court had or could exercise immediately before the passing of this Act are hereby vested in the High Court, and the principal Act shall with the necessary modifications be construed accordingly.
