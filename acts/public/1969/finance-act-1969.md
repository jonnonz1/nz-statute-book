# Finance Act 1969
_Consolidation as at 2017-06-03_

> An Act to make provision with respect to public finance and other matters

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Finance Act 1969.

### 2 Authorising contributions by local authorities to National Heart Foundation of New Zealand
It shall be lawful for any local authority within the meaning of the Local Government Act 2002 to expend out of its general fund or account any sum or sums of money by way of contribution to the National Heart Foundation of New Zealand.
