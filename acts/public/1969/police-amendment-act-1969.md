# Police Amendment Act 1969
_Consolidation as at 2008-10-01_

> An Act to amend the Police Act 1958

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Police Amendment Act 1969, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Police Act 1958 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 8 Repeals and savings
(1) The following enactments are hereby repealed:
  (a) Sections 30A, 73 to 77, 79, and 85 of the principal Act:
  (b) Subsections (1) and (2) of section 2 of the Police Amendment Act 1968.
(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of section 30A of the principal Act, any general instruction issued by the Commissioner under that section shall remain in force until it is replaced by a determination of the Commissioner or a Tribunal order.
(3) Every allowance that was payable immediately before the commencement of this section shall continue to be payable until a determination is made in respect of that allowance.
