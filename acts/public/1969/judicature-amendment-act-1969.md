# Judicature Amendment Act 1969
_Consolidation as at 2018-01-01_

> An Act to amend the Judicature Act 1908

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Judicature Amendment Act 1969, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Judicature Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 4 Appointment of Hon Mr Justice Henry
The Honourable Trevor Ernest Henry, who was appointed to be a Judge of the Supreme Court for a limited period on the 21st day of February 1955 and who was appointed permanently on the 23rd day of May 1955, shall be deemed for the purposes of section 76 of the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956 to have been appointed as a Judge within the meaning of section 75 of that Act on the 21st day of February 1955.
