# Heavy Engineering Research Levy Amendment Act 2012
_Consolidation as at 2012-12-11_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Heavy Engineering Research Levy Amendment Act 2012.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Heavy Engineering Research Levy Act 1978.

### 4 Minister may prescribe rates of research levy
(1) Section 5(2)(a) is amended by omitting $5 and substituting $20.
(2) Section 5(2)(b) is amended by omitting 5 and substituting 10.
