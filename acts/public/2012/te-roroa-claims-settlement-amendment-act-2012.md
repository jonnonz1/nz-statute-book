# Te Roroa Claims Settlement Amendment Act 2012
_Consolidation as at 2012-12-11_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Te Roroa Claims Settlement Amendment Act 2012.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Te Roroa Claims Settlement Act 2008.

### 4 Transfer of Coastal strip: north of Omamari
Section 101 is amended by inserting the following subsection after subsection (2):
  (2A) Subsection (2) applies only to the extent that the marginal strip lies immediately adjacent to the coastline of the Coastal strip: north of Omamari.

### 5 Transfer of Coastal strip: south of Omamari
Section 102 is amended by inserting the following subsection after subsection (2):
  (2A) Subsection (2) applies only to the extent that the marginal strip lies immediately adjacent to the coastline of the Coastal strip: south of Omamari.
