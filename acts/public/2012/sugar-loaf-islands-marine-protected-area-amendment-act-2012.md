# Sugar Loaf Islands Marine Protected Area Amendment Act 2012
_Consolidation as at 2012-02-24_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Sugar Loaf Islands Marine Protected Area Amendment Act 2012.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Sugar Loaf Islands Marine Protected Area Act 1991.

### 4 Protected Area to be conservation area
Section 7(2) is amended by omitting Saddlebank Island and substituting Saddleback Island.
