# War Pensions (Indexation—Budget Measures) Amendment Act 2012
_Consolidation as at 2012-10-23_

### 1 Title
This Act is the War Pensions (Indexation—Budget Measures) Amendment Act 2012.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Section 74C amended (Annual adjustment of rates of veterans’ pensions)
In section 74C(2A) of the War Pensions Act 1954, replace 2011, 1 April 2012, and 1 April 2013 with in any year from 2011 to 2017 (inclusive).
