# Regulatory Reform (Repeals) Act 2012
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-30_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Regulatory Reform (Repeals) Act 2012.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Repeals
The Acts specified in Schedule 1 are repealed.

### 4 Consequential revocations
The regulations specified in Schedule 2 are revoked.

### 5 Repeal of this Act
This Act is repealed on the day that is 28 days after the date on which it comes into force.
