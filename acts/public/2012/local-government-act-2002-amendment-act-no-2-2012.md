# Local Government Act 2002 Amendment Act (No 2) 2012
_Consolidation as at 2012-12-11_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Local Government Act 2002 Amendment Act (No 2) 2012.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Local Government Act 2002.

### 4 Schedule 2 amended
Part 2 of Schedule 2 is amended by omitting the item relating to the Banks Peninsula District Council.
