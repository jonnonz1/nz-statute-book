# Administration Amendment Act 1964
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Administration Act 1952

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Administration Amendment Act 1964, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Administration Act 1952 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
