# Health Amendment Act 1964
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Health Act 1956

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Health Amendment Act 1964, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Health Act 1956 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
