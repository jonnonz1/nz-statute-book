# Social Workers Registration Legislation Amendment Act 2024
_Consolidation as at 2024-02-21_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Social Workers Registration Legislation Amendment Act 2024.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Social Workers Registration Legislation Act 2019.

### 4 Section 2 amended (Commencement)
Replace section 2(2) with:
  (2) Section 24 comes into force on 28 February 2026.
