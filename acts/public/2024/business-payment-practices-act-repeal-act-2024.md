# Business Payment Practices Act Repeal Act 2024
_Consolidation as at 2024-03-07_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Business Payment Practices Act Repeal Act 2024.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Repeal of Business Payment Practices Act 2023 and related matters

### 3 Repeal of Business Payment Practices Act 2023
The Business Payment Practices Act 2023 (2023 No 40) is repealed.

### 4 Revocation of Business Payment Practices Regulations 2023
The Business Payment Practices Regulations 2023 (SL 2023/218) are revoked.

### 5 Principal Act
Section 6 amends the Tax Administration Act 1994.

### 6 Schedule 7 amended
In Schedule 7, Part C, repeal clause 25B.

## Part 2 — Repeal of this Act

### 7 Repeal of this Act
This Act is repealed on the close of the 28th day after the date on which it comes into force.
