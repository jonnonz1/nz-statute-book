# Therapeutic Products Act Repeal Act 2024
_Consolidation as at 2025-01-15_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Therapeutic Products Act Repeal Act 2024.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Repeal of Therapeutic Products Act 2023 and related matters

### 3 Repeal of Therapeutic Products Act 2023
The Therapeutic Products Act 2023 (2023 No 37) is repealed.

**Consequential amendments to Food Act 2014**

### 4 Principal Act
Sections 5 and 6 amend the Food Act 2014.

### 5 Section 413 amended (Overview of transitional provisions)
Repeal section 413(3).

### 6 Section 420 amended (Pre-commencement legislative requirements: Food Act 1981 and regulations)
Replace section 420(3) with:
  (3) The Dietary Supplements Regulations 1985—
    (a) continue in force as if made under this Act; and
    (b) are to be treated as if made under sections 343 and 383 of this Act; and
    (c) may be amended or revoked as if made under this Act.

**Consequential amendments to Dietary Supplements Regulations 1985**

### 7 Principal regulations
Sections 8 and 9 amend the Dietary Supplements Regulations 1985.

### 8 New regulation 2B inserted (Adopted joint food standards do not apply to dietary supplements)
After regulation 2A, insert:Adopted joint food standards do not apply to dietary supplements
  The adopted joint food standards do not apply to dietary supplements.

### 9 Part 3 revoked
Revoke Part 3.

## Part 2 — Repeal of this Act

### 10 Repeal of this Act
This Act is repealed on the close of the 28th day after the date on which it comes into force.
