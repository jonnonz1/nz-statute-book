# Cornish Companies Management Act Repeal Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

> An Act to repeal the Cornish Companies Management Act 1974

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Cornish Companies Management Act Repeal Act 1994.

### 2 Repeal of Cornish Companies Management Act 1974 and amendments
(1) The following enactments are hereby repealed:
  (a) the Cornish Companies Management Act 1974:
  (b) the Cornish Companies Management Amendment Act 1978:
  (c) the Cornish Companies Management Amendment Act 1980.
(2) The Cornish Companies Management Order 1990 is hereby consequentially revoked.
