# Insolvency Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Insolvency Act 1967

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Insolvency Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Insolvency Act 1967 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
