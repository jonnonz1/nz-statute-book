# Sharebrokers Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2011-07-01_

> An Act to amend the Sharebrokers Act 1908

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Sharebrokers Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Sharebrokers Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2
[Repealed]

### 3 Cancellation of registration
The registration under section 9(2) of the principal Act of New Zealand Futures & Options Exchange Limited and of New Zealand Futures & Options Exchange as stock exchanges is hereby cancelled.
