# Law Practitioners Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Law Practitioners Act 1982

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Law Practitioners Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Law Practitioners Act 1982 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
