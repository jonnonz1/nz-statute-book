# Conservation Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Conservation Act 1987

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Conservation Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Conservation Act 1987 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
