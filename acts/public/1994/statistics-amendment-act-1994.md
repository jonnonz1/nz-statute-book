# Statistics Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Statistics Act 1975

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Statistics Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Statistics Act 1975 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Altering name of Department
(1) This subsection amended the Title of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection substituted the definition of the term the Department in the principal Act.
