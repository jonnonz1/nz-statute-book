# Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-18_

> An Act to amend the Sale of Liquor Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Sale of Liquor Act 1989 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
