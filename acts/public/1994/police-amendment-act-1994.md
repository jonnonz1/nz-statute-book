# Police Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Police Act 1958

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Police Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Police Act 1958 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
