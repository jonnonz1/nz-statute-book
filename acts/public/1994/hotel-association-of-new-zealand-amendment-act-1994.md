# Hotel Association of New Zealand Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Hotel Association of New Zealand Act 1969

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Hotel Association of New Zealand Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Hotel Association of New Zealand Act 1969 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 4 Transitional provision as to membership
Notwithstanding the provisions of section 3(2) of the principal Act (as substituted by section 3 of this Act), any person who was a member of the Association immediately before the commencement of this Act shall, subject to this Act, be deemed to continue to be a member after the commencement of this Act.
