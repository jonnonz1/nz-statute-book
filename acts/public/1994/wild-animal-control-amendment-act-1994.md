# Wild Animal Control Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Wild Animal Control Act 1977

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Wild Animal Control Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Wild Animal Control Act 1977 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
