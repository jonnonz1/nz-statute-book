# Te Ture Whenua Maori Amendment Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend Te Ture Whenua Maori Act 1993

### 1 Short Title
(1) This Act may be cited as—
  (a) Te Ture Whenua Maori Amendment Act 1994; or
  (b) The Maori Land Amendment Act 1994.
(2) This Act shall be read together with and deemed part of Te Ture Whenua Maori Act 1993 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
