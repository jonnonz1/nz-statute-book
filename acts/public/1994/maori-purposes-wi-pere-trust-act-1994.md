# Maori Purposes (Wi Pere Trust) Act 1994
_Consolidation as at 1994-06-09_

> An Act to amend the Maori Purposes Act 1991

### 1 Short Title and Commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Maori Purposes (Wi Pere Trust) Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Maori Purposes Act 1991 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on 20 June 1991 (being the date of commencement of the principal Act).

### 2 Wi Pere Trust
Section 2(12) of the principal Act is hereby amended by omitting the expression Part 3, and substituting the expression Parts 2 and 3.
