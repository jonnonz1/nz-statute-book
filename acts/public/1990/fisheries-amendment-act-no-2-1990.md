# Fisheries Amendment Act (No 2) 1990
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Fisheries Act 1983

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Fisheries Amendment Act (No 2) 1990, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Fisheries Act 1983 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Special regulations may relate to freshwater fish farming
(1) This subsection amended section 91(e) of the principal Act.
(2) Until fees for the issue, revocation, renewal, and transfer of licences in respect of the operation of fish farms are in fact prescribed under section 91 of the principal Act, there shall be deemed to be prescribed—inclusive, in each case, of goods and services tax under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1985.
  (a) For the issue, revocation, renewal, or transfer of a licence to process or deal in the produce of a fish farm, a fee of $112.50; and
  (b) For the issue, revocation, renewal, or transfer of any other licence in respect of the operation of a fish farm, a fee of $225;—
(3) The notice fixing fish farm licence fees (Notice No 3171; Ag 9/2/0/2) made on the 21st day of December 1983, and published in the Gazette on the 19th day of January 1984 at page 105, is hereby consequentially revoked.
