# Export Guarantee Amendment Act 1990
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

> An Act to amend the Export Guarantee Act 1964

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Export Guarantee Amendment Act 1990 and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Export Guarantee Act 1964 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on a date to be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.
