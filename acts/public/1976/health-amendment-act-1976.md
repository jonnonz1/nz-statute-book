# Health Amendment Act 1976
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Health Act 1956

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Health Amendment Act 1976, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Health Act 1956 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Regulations as to camping grounds
(1) This subsection inserted section 120B in the principal Act.
(2) The Camping-ground Regulations 1936 and all amendments thereof are hereby declared to be and to have always been validly made.
