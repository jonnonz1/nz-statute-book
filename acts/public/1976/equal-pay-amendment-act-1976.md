# Equal Pay Amendment Act 1976
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Equal Pay Act 1972

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Equal Pay Amendment Act 1976, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Equal Pay Act 1972 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 4 Remuneration of female employees to be increased when rate for male employees increased
(1) This subsection added section 6(9) to the principal Act.
(2) Notwithstanding anything in subsection (1) of this section, no person shall be liable for any offence under section 18 of the principal Act for failing before the passing of this Act to comply with section 6(9) of the principal Act (as added by subsection (1) of this section), if he would not have been so liable if the said section 6(9) had not been enacted.
