# Marriage Amendment Act 1976
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Marriage Act 1955

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Marriage Amendment Act 1976, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Marriage Act 1955 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of January 1977.
