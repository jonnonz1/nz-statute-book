# Ship Registration Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Ship Registration Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Ship Registration Act 1992 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Entitlement to register certain New Zealand-owned ships and other ships
Section 8 of the principal Act is amended by adding the following subsection:
  (3) This section is subject to section 9.
