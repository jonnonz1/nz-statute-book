# Commerce Amendment Act (No 2) 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Commerce Amendment Act (No 2) 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Commerce Act 1986 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Commission may give clearances for business acquisitions
(1) Section 66(3)(a) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the word would, the word not.
(2) Section 66(3)(b) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the word would, the word not.

### 4 Commission may grant authorisations for business acquisitions
Section 67(3)(a) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the word would, the word not.
