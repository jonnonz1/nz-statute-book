# Reserves Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Reserves Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Reserves Act 1977 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Meeting of Boards
Section 32 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (2), and substituting the following subsection:
  (2) An annual meeting of the Board must be held within 2 months after the end of the financial year.

### 4 Unauthorised expenditure
Section 81 of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression $100, and substituting the expression $500.
