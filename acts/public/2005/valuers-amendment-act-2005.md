# Valuers Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Valuers Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Valuers Act 1948 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by omitting the definition of Valuer-General, and substituting the following definition:
  - **Valuer-General** means the Valuer-General appointed under the State Sector Act 1988.
