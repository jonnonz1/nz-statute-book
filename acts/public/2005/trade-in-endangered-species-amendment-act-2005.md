# Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Custody of seized specimens
(1) Section 40(2)(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words this section, and substituting the expression section 39(4).
(2) Section 40(3)(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words this section, and substituting the expression section 39(4).
(3) Section 40(4) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words this section, and substituting the expression section 39.

### 4 Release of seized specimens
Section 41 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the word specimen where it last appears, the words , receptacle, or thing.

### 5 Disposal of seized documents
Section 42(3) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word attendent, and substituting the word attendant.
