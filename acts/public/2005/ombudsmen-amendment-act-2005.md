# Ombudsmen Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Ombudsmen Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Ombudsmen Act 1975 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Evidence
Section 19(5A) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words or the Protected Disclosures Act 2000.
