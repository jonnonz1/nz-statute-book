# Personal Property Securities Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Personal Property Securities Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Personal Property Securities Act 1999 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 16(1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, in its appropriate alphabetical order, the following definition:
  - **Court** or means the High Court of New Zealand.

### 4 Search purposes, etc
Section 173(j) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words court bailiff, and substituting the words bailiff of the High Court of New Zealand or of any other court.

### 5 Application of sections 185 to 189
Section 184(a) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words a court, and substituting the words the High Court of New Zealand or in any other court.
