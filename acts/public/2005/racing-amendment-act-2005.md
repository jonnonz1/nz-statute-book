# Racing Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Racing Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Racing Act 2003 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 5(1) of the principal Act is amended by omitting from the definition of totalisator racing betting the words racing rules, and substituting the words racing betting rules.

### 4 Rules controlling or prohibiting admission to racecourses
Section 34(5)(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word race, and substituting the word racing.
