# Ngai Tahu Claims Settlement Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Ngai Tahu Claims Settlement Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Ngai Tahu Claims Settlement Act 1998 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Vesting of Maranuku site
Section 414 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after subsection (1), the following subsection:
  (1A) The reservation of the Maranuku site as a recreation reserve is revoked.
