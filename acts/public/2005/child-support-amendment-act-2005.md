# Child Support Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Child Support Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Child Support Act 1991 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 26 April 2005.

### 3 Amendments to principal Act in Schedule
The principal Act is amended in the manner set out in the Schedule.
