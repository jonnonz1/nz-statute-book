# Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-18_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 2004 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act is deemed to have come into force on the commencement of section 14 of the principal Act.

### 3 Conditions of off-licences
Section 14 of the principal Act is amended by adding the following subsections:
  (4) This section applies—
    (a) not only to licences issued on or after the commencement of this section; but
    (b) also to licences in force immediately before the commencement of this section.
  (5) Subsection (4) applies to avoid doubt.
