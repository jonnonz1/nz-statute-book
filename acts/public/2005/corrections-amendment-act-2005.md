# Corrections Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Corrections Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Corrections Act 2004 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 3(1) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word section the first time that it occurs, and substituting the word Act.

### 4 Functions of probation officers
Section 25(1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after paragraph (ab), the following paragraphs:.
  (ac) to supervise all offenders released on parole and to ensure that the conditions of parole are complied with:
  (ad) to supervise offenders released on compassionate release under section 41 of the Parole Act 2002 and to ensure that the conditions of release (if any) are complied with:

### 5 Matters to be included in annual report
Section 190(1)(b)(i) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words control and supervision, and substituting the words control or supervision.

### 6 Regulations
Section 200(1)(b) of the principal Act is amended by adding the following subparagraph:.
  (v) controlling officers of probation areas:
