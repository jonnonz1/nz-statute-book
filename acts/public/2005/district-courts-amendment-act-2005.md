# District Courts Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2018-01-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the District Courts Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the District Courts Act 1947 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Renumbering of section
The principal Act is amended by renumbering section 79 (as inserted by section 3 of the District Courts Amendment Act 2002) as section 78A.
