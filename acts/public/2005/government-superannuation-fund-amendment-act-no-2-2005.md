# Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act (No 2) 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act (No 2) 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Right to cease to be contributor under Part 6A
Section 88HA of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after paragraph (a), the following paragraph:.
  (aa) to transfer to the Police Superannuation Scheme (being the superannuation scheme registered with that name under Part 1 of the Superannuation Schemes Act 1989 on 20 November 1992) in accordance with those regulations; or

### 4 Regulations

  (ba) prescribing, for the purposes of section 88HA, the way in which any transfer value is to be determined and paid:
