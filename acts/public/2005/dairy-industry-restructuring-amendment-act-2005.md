# Dairy Industry Restructuring Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Dairy Industry Restructuring Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Dairy Industry Restructuring Act 2001 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Use of redeemable preference shares
(1) Section 104(2) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words the surrender amount, and substituting the words that part of the total value of equities surrendered.
(2) Section 104(3) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words the surrender amount referred to in subsection (2), and substituting the words any surrender amount that falls within the total value of equities covered by subsection (2).
