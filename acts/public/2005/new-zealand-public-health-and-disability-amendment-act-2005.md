# New Zealand Public Health and Disability Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the New Zealand Public Health and Disability Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the New Zealand Public Health and Disability Act 2000 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 New heading and section 57 substituted
The principal Act is amended by repealing section 57 and the heading before that section, and substituting the following heading and section:Crown Health Financing AgencyCHFA
  (1) The body corporate established by section 16 of the Health Sector (Transfers) Act 1993 is continued under the name of the Crown Health Financing Agency (CHFA).
  (2) CHFA is a Crown entity for the purposes of section 7 of the Crown Entities Act 2004.
  (3) The Crown Entities Act 2004 applies to CHFA except to the extent that this Act expressly provides otherwise.
  (4) CHFA is owned by the Crown.

### 4 References to Residual Health Management Unit to be read as references to Crown Health Financing Agency
Unless in any case the context otherwise requires, every reference to the Residual Health Management Unit or RHMU in any enactment or document is, after the commencement of this section, to be read as a reference to the Crown Health Financing Agency or CHFA.

### 5 Consequential amendments
(1) The principal Act is amended in the manner indicated in Part 1 of the Schedule.
(2) The enactments specified in Part 2 of the Schedule are amended in the manner indicated in that Part.
