# Electoral Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Electoral Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Electoral Act 1993 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Election of list candidates
(1) Section 127(4) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words a copy of.
(2) Section 127 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (5), and substituting the following subsection:
  (5) Every consent submitted under subsection (4)—
    (a) must be in a form provided by the Chief Electoral Officer; and
    (b) must be signed by the person concerned; and
    (c) may be submitted by hand, post, or facsimile transmission.
(3) Schedule 2 of the principal Act is consequentially amended by repealing form 5.
