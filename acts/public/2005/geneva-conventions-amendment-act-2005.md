# Geneva Conventions Amendment Act 2005
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Geneva Conventions Amendment Act 2005.
(2) In this Act, the Geneva Conventions Act 1958 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Notice of trial of protected persons to be served on protecting power, etc
Section 4(1)(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words to death or.

### 4 Appeals by protected persons
(1) Section 6(1) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words to death or.
(2) Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsections (5) and (6).
