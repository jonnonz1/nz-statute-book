# Judicature Amendment Act 1980
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Judicature Act 1908

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Judicature Amendment Act 1980, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Judicature Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) Sections 2 and 3 of this Act shall come into force on the 1st day of January 1981.
(3) Except as provided in subsection (2) of this section, this Act shall come into force on the 1st day of May 1981.

### 2 Age of retirement

(2) This subsection amended section 14 of the principal Act.
(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2) of this section, any Judge who is in office at the commencement of this section may, at his option, continue in office until he attains the age of 72.
