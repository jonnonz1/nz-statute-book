# Local Government Amendment Act 1980
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Local Government Act 1974

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government Amendment Act 1980, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Local Government Act 1974 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) Sections 51 and 54 of this Act shall come into force on the day on which this Act receives the Governor-General's assent.
(3) Section 58 of this Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of April 1980.
(4) Except as provided in subsections (2) and (3) of this section, this Act shall come into force on the 28th day after the day on which it receives the Governor-General's assent.

### 2 Interpretation
(1)
(2) Every reference in the principal Act or in any other enactment to the principal officer of a regional council, united council, or territorial authority shall be read as a reference to the principal administrative officer of that council or authority.
