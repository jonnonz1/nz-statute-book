# Westland and Nelson Coalfields Administration Act 1901
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to extend The Westland and Nelson Coalfields Administration Act 1877.

### 1 Short Title
The Short Title of this Act is The Westland and Nelson Coalfields Administration Act 1901; and it shall form part of and be read together with The Westland and Nelson Coalfields Administration Act 1877 (hereinafter called the principal Act).

### 2 Endowment for Westport Harbour Board
The parcel of land described in the Schedule hereto shall be deemed to form part of Schedule 4 to the principal Act, and shall be administered accordingly.

### 3 Board may prospect for coal
The Westport Harbour Board may from time to time apply such portion of its funds as it thinks fit, not exceeding two thousand dollars in any one year, in prospecting for coal on its endowments.
