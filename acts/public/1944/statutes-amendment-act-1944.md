## Part  — Counties insurance empowering

### 13 Management of business of company
(1) This section shall be read together with and deemed part of the Counties Insurance Empowering Act 1941.
(2) Notwithstanding anything in the State Insurance Act 1990, it shall be lawful for the New Zealand Counties Co-operative Insurance Company Limited and the State Insurance General Manager from time to time to enter into agreements for the management of the company's business by the said General Manager, on such terms and subject to such conditions as may be agreed upon between the company and the said General Manager.
(3) While any such agreement as aforesaid remains in force the company's auditor shall, notwithstanding anything in the Companies Act 1955, be the Audit Office, which for that purpose shall have and may exercise all such powers as it has under the Public Finance Act 1977 in respect of public money.
(4) This section shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of January 1944.
