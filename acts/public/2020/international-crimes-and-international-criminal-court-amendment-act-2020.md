# International Crimes and International Criminal Court Amendment Act 2020
_Consolidation as at 2020-08-06_

### 1 Title
This Act is the International Crimes and International Criminal Court Amendment Act 2020.

### 2 Commencement
(1) This section and sections 1, 3, and 6 come into force on the day after the date on which this Act receives the Royal assent.
(2) The rest of this Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.
(3) One or more orders may be made under subsection (2) bringing different provisions into force on different dates.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the International Crimes and International Criminal Court Act 2000 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 4 amended (Interpretation)
(1) In section 4(1), replace the definition of international crime with:
  - **international crime** , in relation to the ICC,—
    (a) means a crime in respect of which the ICC has jurisdiction under Article 5 of the Statute; but
    (b) does not include the crime of aggression, as provided under Articles 8 bis, 15 bis, 15 ter, and 25 (3 bis) (which are provisions of the Statute that do not apply to New Zealand)
(2) In section 4(1), replace the definition of Statute with:
  - **Statute** means the Rome Statute of the ICC dated 17 July 1998, a copy of the English text of which is set out in the Schedule and includes—
    (a) amendments to Article 8 of the Statute contained in depository notifications—
      (i) C.N﻿.﻿533.2010 Treaties-6, dated 29 November 2010; and
      (ii) C.N﻿.﻿116.2018.TREATIES-XVIII﻿.﻿10, dated 8 March 2018; and
      (iii) C.N﻿.﻿125.2018.TREATIES-XVIII﻿.﻿10, dated 8 March 2018; and
      (iv) C.N﻿.﻿126.2018.TREATIES-XVIII﻿.﻿10, dated 8 March 2018; and
      (v) C.N﻿.﻿399.2019.TREATIES-XVIII﻿.﻿10, dated 30 August 2019; and
    (b) amendments to the Statute contained in depository notification C.N﻿.﻿651.2010.TREATIES-8, dated 29 November 2010, including the insertion of the following Articles into the Statute:
      (i) Article 8 bis:
      (ii) Article 15 bis:
      (iii) Article 15 ter:
      (iv) Article 25 (3 bis)

### 5 Section 6 amended (Statute to have force of law)
After section 6(2), insert:
  (3) Despite subsections (1) and (2), Articles 8 bis, 15 bis, 15 ter, and 25 (3 bis) of the Statute, which relate to the crime of aggression, do not apply to, and do not have the force of law in, New Zealand.

### 6 Section 120 amended (Requests involving conflict with other international obligations)
(1) In section 120(2)﻿(c), replace surrender with assistance.
(2) In section 120(3), replace Minister with Attorney-General.

### 7 Schedule replaced
Replace the Schedule with the Schedule set out in the Schedule of this Act.
