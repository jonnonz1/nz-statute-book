# Family Court (Supporting Families in Court) Legislation Act 2020
_Consolidation as at 2020-05-15_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Family Court (Supporting Families in Court) Legislation Act 2020.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 July 2020.

## Part 1 — Amendments to Care of Children Act 2004

### 3 Amendments to Care of Children Act 2004
This Part amends the Care of Children Act 2004.

### 4 Section 7A repealed (Lawyers acting for parties)
Repeal section 7A.

### 5 Section 143 amended (Appeals to High Court)
Repeal section 143(3A)﻿(c).

### 6 Schedule 1AA amended
In Schedule 1AA, after Part 1, insert:Provisions relating to Family Court (Supporting Families in Court) Legislation Act 2020InterpretationLawyers may act in pending proceedings
  In this Part,—
    - **2020 Act** means the Family Court (Supporting Families in Court) Legislation Act 2020
    - **pending proceedings** means any proceedings commenced under this Act before 1 July 2020 but not completed by that date.
  On and from 1 July 2020 a lawyer may act for any party to a pending proceeding (including representing a party at a settlement conference) even though before the repeal on that date of section 7A of this Act (by section 4 of the 2020 Act) a lawyer could not act for the party.

## Part 2 — Amendments to Legal Services Act 2011

### 7 Amendments to Legal Services Act 2011
This Part amends the Legal Services Act 2011.

### 8 New section 4A inserted (Transitional, savings, and related provisions)
After section 4, insert:Transitional, savings, and related provisions
  The transitional, savings, and related provisions set out in Schedule 1AA have effect according to their terms.

### 9 Section 7 amended (Proceedings for which legal aid may be granted: civil matters)
Repeal section 7(3A), (3B), and (6).

### 10 New Schedule 1AA inserted
Insert the Schedule 1AA set out in the Schedule of this Act as the first schedule to appear after the last section of the principal Act.

## Schedule  — New Schedule 1AA inserted

### Schedule 1AA — Transitional, savings, and related provisions

#### Part 1 — Provisions relating to Family Court (Supporting Families in Court) Legislation Act 2020

### 1 Interpretation
In this Part,—
  - **2020 Act** means the Family Court (Supporting Families in Court) Legislation Act 2020
  - **pending proceedings** means any proceedings commenced under the Care of Children Act 2004 before 1 July 2020 but not completed by that date.

### 2 Legal aid may be granted for pending proceedings
Legal aid may be granted under section 7 in respect of pending proceedings even though before the repeal of section 7(3A), (3B), and (6) on 1 July 2020 (by section 9 of the 2020 Act) legal aid could not have been granted in respect of those proceedings.
