# New Zealand Public Health and Disability Amendment Act 2020
_Consolidation as at 2022-07-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the New Zealand Public Health and Disability Amendment Act 2020.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 30 September 2020.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the New Zealand Public Health and Disability Act 2000 (the principal Act).

**Amendment to New Zealand Public Health and Disability Act 2000**

### 4 Part 4A repealed
Repeal Part 4A.

**Consequential amendments**

### 5 Consequential amendments to Support Workers (Pay Equity) Settlement Act 2017
(1) This section amends the Support Workers (Pay Equity) Settlement Act 2017.
(2) In section 5, definition of employer, paragraph (b), delete (for example, under a family care policy within the meaning of that term in section 70B of the New Zealand Public Health and Disability Act 2000).
(3) Repeal section 9A(3).
