# Forests Amendment Act 1972
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Forests Act 1949

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Forests Amendment Act 1972, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Forests Act 1949 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Timber preservation
(1)
(2) This subsection amended section 72(4) of the principal Act.
(3) The Timber Preservation Regulations 1955 shall continue in force and have effect as if they had been enacted under the principal Act and they may be amended or revoked accordingly.
