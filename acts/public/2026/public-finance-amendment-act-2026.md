# Public Finance Amendment Act 2026
_Consolidation as at 2026-04-02_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Public Finance Amendment Act 2026.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 July 2026.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Public Finance Act 1989.

### 20 New section 26MA and cross-heading inserted
After section 26M, insert:Tax expenditure statementTax expenditure statement
  (1) The Minister must present to the House of Representatives a tax expenditure statement prepared by the Treasury for each financial year.
  (2) The Minister must present the tax expenditure statement immediately after they have delivered the Budget for the financial year to which the statement relates, or at any time prior to that time on the same day.
  (3) The tax expenditure statement must include, for each tax expenditure,—
    (a) a description of the tax expenditure; and
    (b) the legislative provision that authorises the tax expenditure; and
    (c) the estimated value of the tax expenditure, where it is reasonably feasible to estimate; and
    (d) the type and objective of the tax expenditure; and
    (e) if available, a link to an internet site where documents that record the following are published:
      (i) the decision to make the tax expenditure:
      (ii) the analysis that informed that decision.
  (4) In this section, tax expenditure includes an exemption, allowance, preferential tax rate, deferral, or offset.
