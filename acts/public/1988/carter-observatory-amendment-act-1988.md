# Carter Observatory Amendment Act 1988
_Consolidation as at 2010-06-01_

> An Act to amend the Carter Observatory Act 1938

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Carter Observatory Amendment Act 1988, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Carter Observatory Act 1938 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of April 1989.

### 2 Constitution of Board
(1) This subsection substituted s 5 of the principal Act.
(2) Section 2 of the Carter Observatory Amendment Act 1981 is hereby consequentially repealed.
(3) Notwithstanding anything in the principal Act, all persons holding office as members of the Board at the expiry of the 31st day of March 1989 shall be deemed to have vacated office with effect from that time.
