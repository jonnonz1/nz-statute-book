# Law Practitioners Amendment Act 1988
_Consolidation as at 2008-08-01_

> An Act to amend the Law Practitioners Act 1982

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Law Practitioners Amendment Act 1988, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Law Practitioners Act 1982 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Solicitors in practice to pay fees into fund
(1) This subsection substituted section 165 of the principal Act.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) of this section, until a fee is fixed under subsection (1) of section 165 of the principal Act (as substituted by subsection (1) of this section), the fee payable under section 165(1) of the principal Act (as originally enacted) immediately before the commencement of this Act shall continue to be payable as if subsection (1) of this section had not been enacted.
