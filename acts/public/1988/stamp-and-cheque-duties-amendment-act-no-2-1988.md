# Stamp and Cheque Duties Amendment Act (No 2) 1988
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Stamp and Cheque Duties Act 1971

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Stamp and Cheque Duties Amendment Act (No 2) 1988, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Stamp and Cheque Duties Act 1971 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 17th day of March 1988.
