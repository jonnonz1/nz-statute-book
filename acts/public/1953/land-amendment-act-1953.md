# Land Amendment Act 1953
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Land Act 1948

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Land Amendment Act 1953, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Land Act 1948 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
