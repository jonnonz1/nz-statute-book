# Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Amendment Act 1920
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Act 1908

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Amendment Act 1920, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Consent required for disposal of Crown-granted land
Notwithstanding anything in section 7 of the principal Act, a society under that Act shall not have authority to sell or exchange any lands derived by it from the Crown by way of grant or otherwise, whether derived before or after the passing of this Act, except with the precedent consent of the Governor-General in Council.
