# War Pensions Amendment Act 1966
_Consolidation as at 2014-12-07_

> An Act to amend the War Pensions Act 1954

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the War Pensions Amendment Act 1966, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the War Pensions Act 1954 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
