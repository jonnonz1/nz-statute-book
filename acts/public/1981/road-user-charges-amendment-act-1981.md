# Road User Charges Amendment Act 1981
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-01_

> An Act to amend the Road User Charges Act 1977 and other related Acts

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Road User Charges Amendment Act 1981, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Road User Charges Act 1977 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) Except as provided by subsection (3) of this section, this Act shall come into force on the 1st day of April 1982, or such earlier date as may be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.
(3) Sections 1, 3, and 4 of this Act shall come into force on the day on which this Act receives the Governor-General's assent.
