# Petroleum Demand Restraint (Regulations Validation and Revocation) Act 1981
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

> An Act to validate and confirm regulations made under the Petroleum Demand Restraint Act 1981 and to revoke certain regulations continued in force by that Act

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Petroleum Demand Restraint (Regulations Validation and Revocation) Act 1981.

### 2 Confirmation
The regulations specified in Schedule 1 are hereby validated and confirmed, subject to the modification made by section 3.

### 3 Modification of regulations
(1) Amendment(s) incorporated in the regulations.
(2) The regulation 4A inserted into the Petroleum Demand Restraint (Motor Spirits) Regulations 1981 by subsection (1) shall, subject to subclause (3) of that regulation and to section 9(1) of the Petroleum Demand Restraint Act 1981, come into force on a date to be appointed by Order in Council for the commencement of regulation 4A of those regulations.

### 4 Revocations
The regulations specified in Schedule 2 are hereby revoked.

### 5 Consequential amendments
Amendment(s) incorporated in the Act(s).

### 6 Saving
Nothing in this Act affects the power to amend, suspend, or revoke, under the Petroleum Demand Restraint Act 1981, the regulations specified in Schedule 1 (including the regulation inserted by section 3).
