# Public Works Amendment Act (No 4) 1987
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Public Works Act 1981

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Public Works Amendment Act (No 4) 1987, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Public Works Act 1981 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of April 1988.
