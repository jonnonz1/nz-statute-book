# Social Security Amendment Act 1987
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Social Security Act 1964

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Social Security Amendment Act 1987, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Social Security Act 1964 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 10 Removal of exemption from liable parent scheme
(1) Section 2 of the Social Security Amendment Act 1980 is hereby repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) of this section, if any person, immediately before the commencement of this section, was in receipt of a domestic purposes benefit to which section 2 of the Social Security Amendment Act 1980 applied, the Director-General shall continue to assess and pay the benefits as if subsection (1) of this section had not been enacted until the benefit is first renewed under section 80(4) of the principal Act.
(3) This section shall come into force on the 1st day of October 1987.
