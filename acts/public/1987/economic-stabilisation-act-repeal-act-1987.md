# Economic Stabilisation Act Repeal Act 1987
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

> An Act to repeal the Economic Stabilisation Act 1948 and to make provision incidental thereto

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Economic Stabilisation Act Repeal Act 1987.

### 2 Repeals
The following enactments are hereby repealed, namely:
  (a) the Economic Stabilisation Act 1948:
  (b) the Economic Stabilisation Amendment Act 1968:
  (c) the Economic Stabilisation Amendment Act 1982:
  (d) Part 1 of the Finance Act 1983.

### 3 Consequential amendments and repeals
(1) The enactment specified in Schedule 1 is hereby consequentially amended in the manner indicated in that Schedule.
(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 2 are hereby consequentially repealed.
