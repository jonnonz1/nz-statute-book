# Public Safety Conservation Act Repeal Act 1987
_Consolidation as at 2017-06-03_

> An Act to repeal the Public Safety Conservation Act 1932

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Public Safety Conservation Act Repeal Act 1987.
(2) This Act shall come into force on 1 September 1987.

### 2 Repeal of Public Safety Conservation Act 1932
The following enactments are hereby repealed:
  (a) the Public Safety Conservation Act 1932:
  (b) the Public Safety Conservation Amendment Act 1960:
  (c) section 26(a) of the Petroleum Demand Restraint Act 1981:
  (d) section 84(2) of the Civil Defence Act 1983:
  (e) so much of Schedule 1 of the Constitution Act 1986 as relates to the Public Safety Conservation Act 1932.
