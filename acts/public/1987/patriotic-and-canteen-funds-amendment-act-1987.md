# Patriotic and Canteen Funds Amendment Act 1987
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Act 1947

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Amendment Act 1987, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Act 1947 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of October 1987.

### 6 Savings
Unless the context otherwise requires, every reference in any other Act or in any regulation, rule, order, instrument, notice, or other document whatever to the New Zealand Patriotic Fund Board or to the Canteen Fund Board shall, after the commencement of this Act, be read as a reference to the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Board.

### 7 Transitional provisions
(1) The officers appointed by the New Zealand Patriotic Fund Board pursuant to section 8 of the principal Act (as in force before the commencement of this Act) and by the Canteen Fund Board pursuant to section 30 of the principal Act (as so in force) are hereby deemed to have been appointed by the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Board as officers of that Board pursuant to section 8 of the principal Act (as substituted by section 3 of this Act).
(2) All committees appointed by the New Zealand Patriotic Fund Board pursuant to section 7(c) of the principal Act (as in force before the commencement of this Act) and all committees appointed by the Canteen Fund Board pursuant to section 29(b) of the principal Act (as so in force) are hereby deemed to have been appointed by the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Board as committees of that Board pursuant to section 7(c) of the principal Act (as substituted by section 3 of this Act), and all powers delegated to such committees by the New Zealand Patriotic Fund Board pursuant to the said section 7(c) (as so in force) and by the Canteen Fund Board pursuant to the said section 29(b) (as so in force) shall, so far as they are applicable, be deemed to have been delegated by the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Board pursuant to the said section 7(c) (as so substituted).
(3) The Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the New Zealand Patriotic Fund Board appointed pursuant to section 5 of the principal Act (as in force before the commencement of this Act) shall be deemed to be the Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Patriotic and Canteen Funds Board until such time as that Board appoints a Chairman and Deputy Chairman pursuant to section 5 of the principal Act (as substituted by section 3 of this Act).
