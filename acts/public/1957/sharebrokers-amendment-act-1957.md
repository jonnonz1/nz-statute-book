# Sharebrokers Amendment Act 1957
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Sharebrokers Act 1908

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Sharebrokers Amendment Act 1957, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Sharebrokers Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
