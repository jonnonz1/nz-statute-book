# Gas Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2008-01-18_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Gas Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Gas Act 1992.

### 4 Other gas governance regulations
Section 43G(2) is amended by repealing paragraph (c) and substituting the following paragraph:.
  (c) providing for arrangements to enable consumers to switch gas retailers:
