# Te Ture Whenua Maori Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is—
  (a) Te Ture Whenua Maori Amendment Act 2007; or
  (b) the Maori Land Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Amendments to Te Ture Whenua Maori Act 1993
This Act amends Te Ture Whenua Maori Act 1993.

### 4 Appointment of Judges
Section 7(4) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 5 Appointment of temporary Judges
Section 9(5) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 6 Tenure of office
Section 12(2) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
