# Civil Union Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Civil Union Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Civil Union Act 2004.

### 4 Registrar may issue certificate of no impediment
The heading to section 25 is amended by omitting Registrar and substituting Registrar-General.
