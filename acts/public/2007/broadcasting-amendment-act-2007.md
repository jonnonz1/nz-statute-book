# Broadcasting Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Broadcasting Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Broadcasting Act 1989.

### 4 Interpretation
(1) The definition of total operating revenue in section 2(1) is repealed.
(2) Section 2(1) is amended by inserting the following definition in its appropriate alphabetical order:
  - **total revenue** means all income classified as revenue by the applicable financial reporting standard approved under section 24 of the Financial Reporting Act 1993.

### 5 Return on total operating revenue
(1) The heading to section 30A is amended by omitting operating.
(2) Section 30A(1) is amended by omitting operating.
(3) Section 30A(2)(b) is amended by omitting operating.
(4) Section 30A(3) is amended by omitting operating.
(5) Section 30A(4) is amended by omitting operating in each place where it appears.

### 6 Payment of annual levy
Section 30B(1) is amended by omitting operating.

### 7 Annual levy
Section 30C is amended by omitting operating.
