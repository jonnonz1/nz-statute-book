# Legal Services Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Legal Services Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Legal Services Act 2000.

### 4 Proceedings for which legal aid may be granted: civil matters
Section 7(4)(f) as amended by the Legal Services Amendment Act 2006 is amended by omitting Residence Appeal Board and substituting Residence Review Board.

### 5 Other situations where legal aid refused or limited: civil matters
Section 10(1)(b) is amended by omitting limited purposes permit and substituting limited purpose permit.
