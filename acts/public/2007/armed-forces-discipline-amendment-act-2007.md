# Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971.

### 4 Amendments to principal Act in Schedule
The principal Act is amended in the manner set out in the Schedule.
