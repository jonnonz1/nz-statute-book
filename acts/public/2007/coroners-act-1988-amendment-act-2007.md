# Coroners Act 1988 Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-07-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Coroners Act 1988 Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Amendment to Coroners Act 1988
This Act amends the Coroners Act 1988.

### 4 Retirement of coroners and deputies
Section 33(1) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
