# Taxation (Annual Rates of Income Tax 2007–08) Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2008-05-29_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Taxation (Annual Rates of Income Tax 2007–08) Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Rates of income tax for 2007–08 tax year
(1) Income tax imposed by section BB 1 of the Income Tax Act 2004 must, for the 2007–08 tax year, be paid at the basic rates specified in Schedule 1 of that Act.
(2) The Taxation (Annual Rates of Income Tax 2006–07) Act 2006 is repealed.
