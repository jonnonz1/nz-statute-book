# Status of Children Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Status of Children Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Status of Children Act 1969.

### 4 Interpretation
The definition of AHR procedure in section 14(1) is amended by omitting section 14A and substituting section 15.
