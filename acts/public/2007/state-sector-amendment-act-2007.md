# State Sector Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the State Sector Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the State Sector Act 1988.

### 4 Duty to act independently
Section 33 is amended by omitting but subject to sections 51 and 52 of this Act,.
