# Prisoners' and Victims' Claims Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2012-06-30_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Prisoners' and Victims' Claims Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act is deemed to have come into force on 30 June 2007.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Prisoners' and Victims' Claims Act 2005.

### 4 Expiry of subpart
[Repealed]

### 5 Compensation of prisoners, etc, must be paid to Secretary
[Repealed]

### 6 Continuation of subpart 1 of Part 2
Despite section 16 of the principal Act (as in force immediately before the commencement of this Act), subpart 1 of Part 2 of the principal Act is deemed to have continued in force after 30 June 2007.
