# District Courts Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2018-01-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the District Courts Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Amendments to District Courts Act 1947
This Act amends the District Courts Act 1947.

### 4 Tenure of office
Section 7(2) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 5 Acting Judges
Section 10(2) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 6 Retired Judges
Section 10A(1) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 7 Tenure of office
Section 11F(1) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 8 Retired Community Magistrates
Section 11H(1) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
