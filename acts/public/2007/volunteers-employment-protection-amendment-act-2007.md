# Volunteers Employment Protection Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Volunteers Employment Protection Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Volunteers Employment Protection Act 1973.

### 4 Evidence
Section 14(3) is amended by omitting Chief of Staff and substituting Chief of Service.
