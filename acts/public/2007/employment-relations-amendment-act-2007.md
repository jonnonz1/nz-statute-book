# Employment Relations Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Employment Relations Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Amendments to Employment Relations Act 2000
This Act amends the Employment Relations Act 2000.

### 4 Age of retirement
Section 205 is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 5 Appointment of temporary Judges
(1) Section 207(2) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
(2) Section 207(4) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
