# Industrial and Provident Societies Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Industrial and Provident Societies Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1908.

### 4 Registry of societies
Section 5(b) is amended by omitting 3 written or printed copies and substituting a written or printed copy.

### 5 Rules and amendments
Section 7(b) is amended by omitting 3 copies and substituting a copy.
