# Judicature Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2018-01-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Judicature Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Amendments to Judicature Act 1908
This Act amends the Judicature Act 1908.

### 4 Age of retirement
Section 13 is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 5 Rights on retirement before attaining retiring age
Section 14 is amended by omitting 68 in each place where it appears and substituting in each case 70.

### 6 Vacation of office
Section 26E(3) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 7 Temporary Associate Judges
Section 26H(3) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
