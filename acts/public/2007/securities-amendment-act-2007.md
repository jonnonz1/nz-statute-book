# Securities Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2014-12-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Securities Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Securities Act 1978.

### 4 Interpretation
Paragraph (a) of the definition of Registrar in section 2(1) is amended by omitting sections 7 and 67 and substituting sections 67A to 68B, 68G, 68I, and 68K.

### 5 Civil liability for breach of contributory mortgage regulations
Section 57A(2)(c) is amended by omitting District Registrar of Companies at Wellington and substituting Registrar.
