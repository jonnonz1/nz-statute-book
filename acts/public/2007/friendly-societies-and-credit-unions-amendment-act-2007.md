# Friendly Societies and Credit Unions Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Friendly Societies and Credit Unions Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Friendly Societies and Credit Unions Act 1982.

### 4 Applications for registration of societies
Section 12(2) is amended by omitting at least 2 copies and substituting a copy.

### 5 Branches to be registered concurrently with society
Section 14(1)(d) is amended by omitting two copies and substituting a copy.

### 6 Revising Barrister may examine rules
Section 16(1) is amended by omitting copies and substituting copy.

### 7 Acknowledgement of registration
(1) Section 17(1)(b) is repealed.
(2) Section 17(3) is amended by omitting and publish, by notice in the Gazette, details of the amendment.

### 8 Amendment of rules
Section 23(1) is amended by omitting 2 copies and substituting a copy.

### 9 Conversion of society into branch
Section 88(4)(b) is amended by—
  (a) omitting two copies and substituting a copy ; and
  (b) omitting a copy of.

### 10 Application for registration of Association
Section 145(2) is amended by omitting at least 2 copies and substituting a copy.
