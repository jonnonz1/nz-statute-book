# Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Act 1993.

### 4 Conduct of reviews
Section 52(4) is amended by omitting section 3 of this Act and substituting sections 3 to 3D.
