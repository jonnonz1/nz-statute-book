# Charitable Trusts Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Charitable Trusts Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Charitable Trusts Act 1957.

### 4 Applications for incorporation
Section 10(1) is repealed and the following subsection substituted:
  (1) Each subscriber to an application for incorporation must add the subscriber's name and address to the subscriber's signature.
