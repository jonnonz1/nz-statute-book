# Retirement Villages Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Retirement Villages Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Retirement Villages Act 2003.

### 4 Schedule 5 amended
Schedule 5 is amended by omitting from the first line included and substituting given effect to.
