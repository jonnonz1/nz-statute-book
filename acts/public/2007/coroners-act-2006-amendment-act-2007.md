# Coroners Act 2006 Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Coroners Act 2006 Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 July 2007.

### 3 Amendments to Coroners Act 2006
This Act amends the Coroners Act 2006.

### 4 Coroners
(1) Section 103(4) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
(2) Section 103(5) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.

### 5 Relief coroners
(1) Section 104(5) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
(2) Section 104(6) is amended by omitting 68 and substituting 70.
