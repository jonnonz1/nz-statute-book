# Social Welfare (Transitional Provisions) Amendment Act 2007
_Consolidation as at 2007-11-05_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Social Welfare (Transitional Provisions) Amendment Act 2007.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Social Welfare (Transitional Provisions) Act 1990.

### 4 Membership of Board
Section 46(1)(b) is amended by omitting on the nomination of the New Zealand War Amputees Association Incorporated and substituting to represent the interests of war amputees.
