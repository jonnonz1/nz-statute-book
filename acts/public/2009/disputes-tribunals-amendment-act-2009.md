# Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 2009
_Consolidation as at 2009-07-27_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 2009.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 August 2009.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Disputes Tribunals Act 1988.

## Part 1 — Amendments to principal Act

### 4 Jurisdiction of Tribunals
Section 10(1A) and (3) are amended by omitting $7,500 and substituting in each case $15,000.

### 5 Extension of jurisdiction by agreement between the parties
Section 13(2) is amended by omitting $12,000 and substituting $20,000.

### 6 Abandonment to bring claim within jurisdiction
Section 14 is amended by omitting $7,500 and substituting $15,000.

### 7 Orders of Tribunal
(1) Section 19(5), (6), and (7) are amended by omitting $7,500 in each place where it appears and substituting in each case $15,000.
(2) Section 19(7) is amended by omitting $12,000 and substituting $20,000.

## Part 2 — Consequential amendments

### 8 Consequential amendments
The enactments listed in the Schedule are amended in the manner set out in that schedule.
