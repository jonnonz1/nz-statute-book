# Sentencing Amendment Act (No 3) 2009
_Consolidation as at 2009-11-02_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Sentencing Amendment Act (No 3) 2009.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 December 2009.

### 3 Sentencing Act 2002 amended
This Act amends the Sentencing Act 2002.

### 4 Aggravating and mitigating factors
