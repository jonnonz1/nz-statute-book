# Bills of Exchange Amendment Act 1995
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Bills of Exchange Act 1908

### 1 Short Title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Bills of Exchange Amendment Act 1995, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Bills of Exchange Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
