# Criminal Justice Amendment Act 1995
_Consolidation as at 2013-07-01_

> An Act to amend the Criminal Justice Act 1985

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Criminal Justice Amendment Act 1995, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Criminal Justice Act 1985 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on a date to be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.
