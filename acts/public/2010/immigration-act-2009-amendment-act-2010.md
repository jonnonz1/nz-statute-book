# Immigration Act 2009 Amendment Act 2010
_Consolidation as at 2010-04-08_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Immigration Act 2009 Amendment Act 2010.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Immigration Act 2009.

### 4 Commencement
(1) Section 2(1) is amended by omitting (4) and substituting (5).
(2) Section 2 is amended by adding the following subsection:
  (5) Section 474 comes into force on the day after the date on which the Immigration Act 2009 Amendment Act 2010 receives the Royal assent.
