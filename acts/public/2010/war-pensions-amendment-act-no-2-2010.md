# War Pensions Amendment Act (No 2) 2010
_Consolidation as at 2014-12-07_

### 1 Title
This Act is the War Pensions Amendment Act (No 2) 2010.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 27 September 2010.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the War Pensions Act 1954.

### 4 Interpretation
The definition of Income Test 3 in section 67(1) is amended by omitting $80 and substituting $100.

### 5 Abatement
(1) Section 74D(3)(a) and (4)(a) are amended by omitting $80 and substituting in each case $100.
(2) Section 74D is amended by omitting $180 in each place where it appears and substituting in each case $200.
