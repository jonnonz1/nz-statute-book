# Tariff (New Zealand–Hong Kong, China Closer Economic Partnership Agreement) Amendment Act 2010
_Consolidation as at 2011-01-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Tariff (New Zealand–Hong Kong, China Closer Economic Partnership Agreement) Amendment Act 2010.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.

### 3 Principal document amended
This Act, in accordance with section 9F(1) of the Tariff Act 1988, amends the Tariff.

### 4 Note 3 amended
Note 3 is amended by inserting the following item in its appropriate alphabetical order of preferential abbreviation: Hong Kong, ChinaHK
