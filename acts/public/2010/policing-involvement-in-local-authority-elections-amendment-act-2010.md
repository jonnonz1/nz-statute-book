# Policing (Involvement in Local Authority Elections) Amendment Act 2010
_Consolidation as at 2010-06-30_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Policing (Involvement in Local Authority Elections) Amendment Act 2010.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Policing Act 2008.

### 4 Police involvement in elections
(1) Section 99(1) is amended by omitting or a member of a local authority.
(2) Section 99(1)(b) is amended by omitting or subsections (2) to (4) of this section.
(3) Section 99(2) to (4) are repealed.

### 5 Section 115 repealed
Section 115 is repealed.
