# District Court (District Court Judges) Amendment Act 2025
_Consolidation as at 2025-03-12_

### 1 Title
This Act is the District Court (District Court Judges) Amendment Act 2025.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 13 March 2025.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the District Court Act 2016.

### 4 Section 12 amended (Maximum number of Judges)
(1) In section 12(1), replace 182 with 183.
(2) In section 12(2)﻿(c), replace 181.5 with 182.5.
