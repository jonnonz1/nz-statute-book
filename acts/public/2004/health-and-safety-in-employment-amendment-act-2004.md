# Health and Safety in Employment Amendment Act 2004
_Consolidation as at 2016-04-04_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Health and Safety in Employment Amendment Act 2004.
(2) In this Act, the Health and Safety in Employment Act 1992 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 December 2004.

### 3 Minister may approve occupational health and safety training
Section 19G(1) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words , by notice in the Gazette,.
