# Courts Martial Appeals Amendment Act 2004
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Courts Martial Appeals Amendment Act 2004.
(2) In this Act, the Courts Martial Appeals Act 1953 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Constitution of Courts Martial Appeal Court
Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by adding the following subsection:
  (4) Every person appointed under subsection (1)(b) has all the immunities of a Judge of the High Court for the purposes of carrying out his or her tasks and functions as a Judge of the Courts Martial Appeal Court.
