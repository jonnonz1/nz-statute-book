# Construction Contracts Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Construction Contracts Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Construction Contracts Act 2002 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Application of other enactments to adjudications
Section 71(2)(a) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression 24, and substituting the expression 32.
