# Customs and Excise (Alcoholic Beverages) Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2018-10-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Customs and Excise (Alcoholic Beverages) Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Customs and Excise Act 1996 is called the principal Act.

## Part 1 — Preliminary provisions

### 2 Commencement
(1) Sections 5 and 6 and Schedule 2 come into force on 1 June 2003.
(2) The rest of the Act comes into force on the day on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Purpose
The purpose of this Act is—
  (a) to align the duty payable on beverages containing more than 14% but not more than 23% alcohol by volume with that payable on beverages exceeding 23% alcohol by volume; and
  (b) to increase the rates of excise duty and excise-equivalent duty payable on alcoholic beverages in line with the preceding 12 months' movement in the Consumers Price Index All Groups excluding credit services.
