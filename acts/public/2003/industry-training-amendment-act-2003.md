# Industry Training Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Industry Training Amendment Act 2003.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Industry Training Amendment Act 2002 amended
Section 7(4) of the Industry Training Amendment Act 2002 is amended by omitting the expression 7(j), and substituting the expression 7(i).
