# Local Government (Rating) Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Local Government (Rating) Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Local Government (Rating) Act 2002 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Regulations relating to rating of educational establishments
Section 25 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (2), and substituting the following subsection:
  (2) Regulations—
    (a) may not be made before 1 July 2005; and
    (b) may be made only on the recommendation of the Minister; and
    (c) may be made only after a report that complies with section 26.

### 4 Report of Ministerial review of rating practice in relation to educational establishments
(1) Section 26(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words the financial years commencing on 1 July 2003 and 1 July 2004, and substituting the words not fewer than 2 consecutive financial years.
(2) Section 26(c)(i) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words administration of, the words Part 8 of.
