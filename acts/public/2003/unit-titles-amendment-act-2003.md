# Unit Titles Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Unit Titles Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Unit Titles Act 1972 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Cancellation of plan on application of proprietors
Section 45(8) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words , and to the Valuer-General,.
