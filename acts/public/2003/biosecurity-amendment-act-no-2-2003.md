# Biosecurity Amendment Act (No 2) 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Biosecurity Amendment Act (No 2) 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Biosecurity Act 1993 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 30 October 2003.

### 3 New section 28B inserted
The principal Act is amended by inserting, after section 28A, the following section:Biosecurity clearance for certain new organisms and qualifying organisms
  Section 28 does not apply to—
    (a) a new organism that is subject to a conditional release approval granted under section 38C of the Hazardous Substances and New Organisms Act 1996; or
    (b) a qualifying organism approved for importation for release with controls under section 38I of that Act.
