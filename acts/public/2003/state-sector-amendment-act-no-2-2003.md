# State Sector Amendment Act (No 2) 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the State Sector Amendment Act (No 2) 2003.
(2) In this Act, the State Sector Act 1988 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by omitting from paragraph (a)(ii) of the definition of the term employer the words Part 9 of.
