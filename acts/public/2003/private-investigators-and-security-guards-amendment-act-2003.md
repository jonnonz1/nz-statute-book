# Private Investigators and Security Guards Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2011-04-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Private Investigators and Security Guards Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Private Investigators and Security Guards Act 1974 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Grounds for cancellation of licence
Section 58(1)﻿(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words Part 3 of the Dogs Registration Act 1955, and substituting the words the Dog Control Act 1996.
