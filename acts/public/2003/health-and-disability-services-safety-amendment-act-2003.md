# Health and Disability Services (Safety) Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Health and Disability Services (Safety) Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Health and Disability Services (Safety) Act 2001 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Limitations on licensing and registration after 30 September 2002
Section 57(4) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word January, and substituting the word October.
