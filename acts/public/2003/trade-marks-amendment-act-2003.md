# Trade Marks Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Trade Marks Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Trade Marks Act 2002 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Status of trade marks registered before commencement of Act
Section 208(2) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression 74, and substituting the expression 73.
