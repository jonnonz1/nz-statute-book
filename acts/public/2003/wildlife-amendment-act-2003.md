# Wildlife Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Wildlife Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Wildlife Act 1953 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Wildlife refuges
Section 14(1)(e) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression Reserves Act 1977, and substituting the expression Conservation Act 1987.
