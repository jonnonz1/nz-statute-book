# Electricity Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Electricity Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Electricity Act 1992 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 2(1) of the principal Act is amended by omitting from paragraph (b) of the definition of electricity operator the words by the Governor-General by Order in Council.
