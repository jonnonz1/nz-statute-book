# Queen Elizabeth the Second National Trust Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Queen Elizabeth the Second National Trust Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Queen Elizabeth the Second National Trust Act 1977 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Meetings of Board
Section 9(4) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words persons specified in paragraphs (a) to (f) of subsection (5) of this section, and substituting the words Director General of Conservation.
