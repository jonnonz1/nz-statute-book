# Business Development Boards Act Repeal Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Business Development Boards Act Repeal Act 2003.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Business Development Boards Act 1991 repealed
The Business Development Boards Act 1991 (1991 No 108) is repealed.

### 4 Consequential amendments to other Acts
(1) Schedule 1 of the Ombudsmen Act 1975 is amended by omitting from Part 2 the item Business Development Boards established pursuant to the Business Development Boards Act 1991.
(2) Schedules 4, 5 and 7 of the Public Finance Act 1989 are amended by omitting from each the item Business Development Boards.
