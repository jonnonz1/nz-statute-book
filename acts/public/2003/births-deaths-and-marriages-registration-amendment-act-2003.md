# Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Act 1995 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Schedule 1A amended
Schedule 1A of the principal Act is amended by inserting, in the appropriate alphabetical order, the following item:Accident Compensation CorporationBirth informationTo verify the identity of a claimant, and a claimant's eligibility or continuing eligibility for a benefitMarriage informationTo verify a claimant's change of nameDeath informationTo identify deceased claimants
