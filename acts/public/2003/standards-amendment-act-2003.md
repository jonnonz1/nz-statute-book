# Standards Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2016-03-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Standards Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Standards Act 1988 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.
