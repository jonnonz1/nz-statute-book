# Health Research Council Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Health Research Council Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Health Research Council Act 1990 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Term of office
Section 9(1) of the principal Act is amended by adding the words or for a shorter term.
