# Ombudsmen Amendment Act 2003
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Ombudsmen Amendment Act 2003.
(2) In this Act, the Ombudsmen Act 1975 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 House of Representatives may make rules for guidance of Ombudsmen
Section 15(1) and (2) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987, and substituting in each case the words under the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987 or under the Protected Disclosures Act 2000.

### 4 Evidence
Section 19(5A) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987, the words or the Protected Disclosures Act 2000.

### 5 Proceedings privileged
(1) Section 26(1)(a) and (b) and (3) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987, the words or the Protected Disclosures Act 2000.
(2) Section 26(4) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987,, the words or under the Protected Disclosures Act 2000,.

### 6 Annual report
Section 29 of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words or the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987, and substituting the words and the Local Government Official Information and Meetings Act 1987 and the Protected Disclosures Act 2000.
