# Wellington City Reserves Act 1871
_Consolidation as at 2017-06-03_

> An Act to provide for the Management of certain parcels of land in the City of Wellington.

## Preamble
Whereas by grant from the Crown dated 3 June 1861 the land firstly described in Schedule 1 annexed and hereinafter referred to as the Town Belt was granted to Isaac Earl Featherston then Superintendent of the Province of Wellington in trust for purposes of public utility to the Town of Wellington and its inhabitants And whereas by another grant from the Crown dated the said 3 June 1861 the land secondly described in the said Schedule 1 annexed and hereinafter referred to as the Canal and Basin Reserve was also granted to the said Isaac Earl Featherston Superintendent as aforesaid in trust for purposes of public utility to the Town of Wellington and its inhabitants:
And whereas by an Act of the Superintendent and Provincial Council of the Province of Wellington intituled An Act for the better management of certain parcels of land in the City of Wellington certain provisions were made for the management of the lands granted as aforesaid And whereas it is expedient that the said recited Act should be repealed And whereas by grant from the Crown dated 27 October 1852 the land described in Schedule 2 annexed and hereinafter referred to as the Wesleyan Reserve was granted to James Watkin Superintendent of Wesleyan Missions for the Province of Wellington and his successors in that office And whereas the said James Watkin as such grantee acting with the consent of the governing body of the Wesleyan Denomination entered into arrangements with the said Isaac Earl Featherston as such Superintendent as aforesaid for the sale to him of the said land so granted by the said lastly recited deed of grant but such arrangements were never fully carried into effect And whereas doubts have arisen whether the said James Watkin can make a valid title to the said land and it is expedient to remove such doubts And whereas it is expedient to make provision for the future management as well of the said lands described in Schedule 1 annexed as of the said Wesleyan Reserve when the same shall have been conveyed to the Superintendent for the time being of the said Province of Wellington as hereinafter provided:

### 1 Short Title
The Short Title of this Act shall be the Wellington City Reserves Act 1871.

### 2 Repeal of Act
The hereinbefore recited Act of the Superintendent and Provincial Council of the Province of Wellington is hereby repealed saving nevertheless to all persons any rights which they may have acquired thereunder.

### 3 Land in Schedule 2 to be conveyed to the Superintendent
For the purpose of carrying into effect any arrangement for the sale or conveyance of the Wesleyan Reserve to the Superintendent for the time being of the said Province of Wellington the said James Watkin his heirs or assigns shall within 6 calendar months after the passing of this Act upon payment of the balance of the purchase money convey to the said Superintendent and his successors the land described in Schedule 2 annexed and from and after the execution of such conveyance the said land shall be deemed to be absolutely freed and discharged from all trusts theretofore affecting the same.

### 5 Superintendent to convey to Botanic Garden Governors part of land in Schedule 2
The Superintendent for the time being of the said Province of Wellington may with the advice and consent of his Executive Council convey to the Governors of the Botanic Gardens created under the provisions of the Botanic Gardens Act 1869 [Repealed] all or any part of the land described in Schedule 2 hereunto annexed to hold the same unto the said Board of Governors upon trust for such purposes of public utility to the City of Wellington and its inhabitants and with such powers as shall in and by the deed or deeds of conveyance thereof be expressed and declared.
