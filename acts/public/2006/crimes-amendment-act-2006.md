# Crimes Amendment Act 2006
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Crimes Amendment Act 2006.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Crimes Act 1961.

### 4 Warrant to collect fine or other sum of money ordered to be paid
(1) Section 19C(2) is amended by inserting or bailiff after constable.
(2) Section 19C is amended by adding the following subsection:
  (3) In this section and section 19D, bailiff means a bailiff of a District Court; and includes any deputy bailiff and any person acting as bailiff under section 15 of the District Courts Act 1947.

### 5 Execution
Section 19D is amended by inserting or bailiff after constable.
