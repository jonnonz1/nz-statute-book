# Meat Board Amendment Act 2006
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Meat Board Amendment Act 2006.

### 2 Commencement
This Act is deemed to have come into force on 1 July 2004.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Meat Board Act 2004.

### 4 Requirements for allocation mechanisms
Section 24 is amended by adding the following subsection:
  (5) To avoid doubt, an allocation mechanism may provide for an allocation of access to quota markets to any person who is a registered exporter, whether or not the person currently exports meat products.

### 5 Decisions
Section 32(1) is amended by inserting eligible after or processing of meat products.

### 6 Fees in relation to quota management systems
(1) Section 36(1) is amended by omitting by meat exporters and substituting by persons applying for or allocated quota.
(2) Section 36(3) is amended by omitting meat exporters and substituting registered exporters.
