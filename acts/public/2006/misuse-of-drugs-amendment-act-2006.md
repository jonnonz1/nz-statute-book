# Misuse of Drugs Amendment Act 2006
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Misuse of Drugs Amendment Act 2006.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Misuse of Drugs Amendment Act 1978.

### 4 Enforcement of fines imposed in High Court
Section 43 is amended by adding, as subsection (2), the following subsection:
  (2) In this section and section 46, Registrar—
    (a) means any Registrar of the High Court or of the District Court; and
    (b) includes any Deputy Registrar.
