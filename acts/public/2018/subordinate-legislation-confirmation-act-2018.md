# Subordinate Legislation Confirmation Act 2018
_Consolidation as at 2018-06-19_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Subordinate Legislation Confirmation Act 2018.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Purpose
The purpose of this Act is to confirm the Civil Aviation (Safety) Levies Amendment Order 2017, which is a confirmable instrument that, unless earlier confirmed by an Act of Parliament, will be revoked at the close of 30 June 2018 under section 47C(1)﻿(a) of the Legislation Act 2012.

### 4 Act binds the Crown
This Act binds the Crown.

### 5 Civil Aviation (Safety) Levies Amendment Order 2017 confirmed
The Civil Aviation (Safety) Levies Amendment Order 2017 (LI 2017/108) is confirmed.
