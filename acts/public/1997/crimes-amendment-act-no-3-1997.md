# Crimes Amendment Act (No 3) 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Crimes Act 1961

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Crimes Amendment Act (No 3) 1997, and is part of the Crimes Act 1961 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 2 Application
This Act applies to any trial for which a jury is constituted on or after the date on which this Act comes into force.

### 3 Discharge of jury
This section substituted s 374(3) and (4), inserted s 374(4A), and substituted s 374(5) of the principal Act
