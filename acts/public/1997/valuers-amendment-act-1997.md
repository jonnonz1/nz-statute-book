# Valuers Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Valuers Act 1948

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Valuers Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Valuers Act 1948 (the principal Act).

### 2 Functions of Institute
This section substituted s 10 of the principal Act.

### 3 Membership of Institute
This section substituted s 11(3) and inserted s 11(4) to (6) of the principal Act.

### 4 Rules of Institute
Section 16(1)(m) of the principal Act (as amended by section 7 of the Decimal Currency Act 1964) is amended by omitting the expression $10, and substituting the expression $500.
