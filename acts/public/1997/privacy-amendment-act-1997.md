# Privacy Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Privacy Act 1993

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Privacy Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Privacy Act 1993 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 2 Protection and privileges of witnesses, etc
This section inserted s 94(1A) and (1B) of the principal Act.
