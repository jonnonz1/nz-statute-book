# Child Support Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Child Support Act 1991

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Child Support Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Child Support Act 1991 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on 1 January 1998.

### 2 Interpretation
This section added paragraph (c)(v) of the definition of the term social security benefit in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
