# Transit New Zealand Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Transit New Zealand Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Transit New Zealand Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Transit New Zealand Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Procedure for obtaining refund
This section amended s 102(5) of the principal Act.
