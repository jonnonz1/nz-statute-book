# Primary Products Marketing Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Primary Products Marketing Act 1953

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Primary Products Marketing Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Primary Products Marketing Act 1953 (the principal Act).

### 2 Regulations
This section inserted s 3(7) to (11) of the principal Act.
