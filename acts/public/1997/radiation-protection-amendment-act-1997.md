# Radiation Protection Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2017-03-07_

> An Act to amend the Radiation Protection Act 1965

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Radiation Protection Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Radiation Protection Act 1965 (the principal Act).

### 2 Offences
(1) Section 26(2) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression $200, and substituting the expression $10,000.
(2) Section 26(2) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression $10, and substituting the expression $500.
