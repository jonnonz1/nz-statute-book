# Treasurer (Statutory References) Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-31_

> An Act to recognise the role of Treasurer in finance legislation

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Treasurer (Statutory References) Act 1997.

### 2 Finance legislation amended to insert references to Treasurer
The enactments listed in the first column of the Schedule are amended in the manner indicated in the second column of the schedule.
