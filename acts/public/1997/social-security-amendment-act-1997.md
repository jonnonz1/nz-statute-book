# Social Security Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Social Security Act 1964

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Social Security Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Social Security Act 1964 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on 1 July 1997.

### 2 Interpretation
This section amended para (c) of the definition of the term accommodation costs in s 61E(1) of the principal Act.

### 3 Rates of benefits, etc, may be increased by Order in Council
(1) This subsection amended s 61H(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 61H(2) of the principal Act.
(3) This subsection inserted s 61H(7) of the principal Act.

### 4 Schedule 18 amended
This section and the Schedule of this Act amended Part 2 of Schedule 18 of the principal Act.
