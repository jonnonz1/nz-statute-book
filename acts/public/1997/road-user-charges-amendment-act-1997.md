# Road User Charges Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2012-08-01_

> An Act to amend the Road User Charges Act 1977

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Road User Charges Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Road User Charges Act 1977 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection substituted the definition of the term Registrar in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended the definition of the term weight in section 2(1) of the principal Act.
(3) Section 2(3) of the Road User Charges Amendment Act 1988 is consequentially repealed.

### 3 Offences
This section inserted s 23(8) of the principal Act.

### 4 Evidence in proceedings
This section amended s 23A(1) of the principal Act.

### 5 Delegation of chief executive's functions or powers to persons outside Ministry
This section inserted s 23D of the principal Act.
