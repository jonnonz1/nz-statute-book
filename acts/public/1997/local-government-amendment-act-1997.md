# Local Government Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Local Government Act 1974

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Local Government Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Local Government Act 1974 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection amended s 2(1) of the principal by substituting the definition of the term region.
(2) Section 2(6) of the Local Government Amendment Act 1992 is consequentially repealed.
