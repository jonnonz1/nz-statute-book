# New Zealand Council of Law Reporting Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the New Zealand Council of Law Reporting Act 1938

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the New Zealand Council of Law Reporting Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the New Zealand Council of Law Reporting Act 1938 (the principal Act).

### 2 New sections relating to proceedings of Council substituted
This section substituted s 10 and inserted ss 10A to 10D of the principal Act.

### 3 Other amendments to principal Act
(1) Sections 8A(c) and 9(1)(b) of the principal Act are consequentially amended by omitting the word Chairman, and substituting the word Chairperson.
(2) Section 5 of the New Zealand Council of Law Reporting Amendment Act 1964 is consequentially repealed.
