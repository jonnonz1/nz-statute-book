# State Sector Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the State Sector Act 1988 so that it no longer applies to kindergarten associations or their employees

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the State Sector Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the State Sector Act 1988 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.
