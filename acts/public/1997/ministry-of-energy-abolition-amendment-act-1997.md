# Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Act 1989

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Act 1989 (the principal Act).

### 2 Regulations
Section 35(f) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression $1,000, and substituting the expression $10,000.
