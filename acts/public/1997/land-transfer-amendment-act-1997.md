# Land Transfer Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2018-11-12_

> An Act to amend the Land Transfer Act 1952

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Land Transfer Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Land Transfer Act 1952 (the principal Act).

### 2 Execution of documents by corporation
This section substituted s 161 of the principal Act.
