# Companies Reregistration Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Companies Reregistration Act 1993

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Companies Reregistration Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Companies Reregistration Act 1993 (the principal Act).

### 2 New sections inserted
(1) This subsection inserted section 13A and 13B of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended section 14(1) of the principal Act.
