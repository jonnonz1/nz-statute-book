# Health Sector (Transfers) Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Health Reforms (Transitional Provisions) Act 1993

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Health Sector (Transfers) Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Health Sector (Transfers) Act 1993 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent, except for section 3.

### 2 Amendments to Disabled Persons Community Welfare Act 1975
This section amended s 26(2) of the principal Act.

### 3 Repeals
(1) This section amended Part 3 of Schedule 5 of the principal.
(2) This section is deemed to have come into force on 1 July 1993.
