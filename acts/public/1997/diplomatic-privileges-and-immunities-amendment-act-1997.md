# Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Act 1968

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Act 1968 (the principal Act).

### 2 New section inserted
This section inserted s 9AA of the principal Act.
