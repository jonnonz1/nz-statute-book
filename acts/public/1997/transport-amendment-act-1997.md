# Transport Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2011-05-10_

> An Act to amend the Transport Act 1962

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Transport Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Transport Act 1962 (the principal Act).
(2) Except as provided in subsection (3), this Act comes into force on the day on which it receives the Royal assent.
(3) The provisions of sections 2(3), 3, 4, 5(1), 11, 12, and 14 come into force on a date to be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council, and different dates may be appointed for different provisions.

### 2 Interpretation
(1) This subsection inserted the definition of the term Controlling authority in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted the definition of the term Fire brigade in s 2(1) of the principal Act.
(3) Section 2(1) of the principal Act is amended by repealing the definition of the term speeding offence, and substituting the following definition:.
  - **Speeding offence** means an offence which consists solely of exceeding any limit of speed fixed by or under—
    (a) This Act or any regulations made under this Act; or
    (b) Any bylaw made under section 72 of this Act; or
    (c) Any ordinary rule or emergency rule made under Part II of the Land Transport Act 1993:

### 3 Owner liability for stationary vehicle offences

### 4 New sections substituted
(1) The principal Act is amended by repealing sections 52 and 52A (as inserted by section 9(1) of the Transport Amendment Act 1989), and substituting the following sections:Speed limitsSaving of existing speed limits
  Subject to section 53 of this Act, no person may drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding that fixed for the relevant circumstances and locality—
    (a) By a controlling authority in accordance with any ordinary rule or emergency rule made under Part 2 of the Land Transport Act 1993; or
    (b) By the Director under any such rule; or
    (c) By a Minister of the Crown by bylaw made under section 72(1)(j) of this Act; or
    (d) By or under any provision of this Act (including section 52A) or any other Act or any rule or regulation relating to temporary speed limits.
  (1) All speed limits fixed by any Act, regulation, bylaw, or notice in the Gazette before the date on which section 4 of the Transport Amendment Act 1997 comes into force continue to have effect in relation to any road or locality to which they applied before that date until superseded in respect of that road or locality by a speed limit fixed on or after that date by—
    (a) A controlling authority in accordance with any ordinary rule or emergency rule made under Part 2 of the Land Transport Act 1993; or
    (b) The Director under any such rule; or
    (c) A Minister of the Crown by bylaw made under section 72(1)(j) of this Act.
(2) Section 9(1) of the Transport Amendment Act 1989 is consequentially repealed.

### 5 Exemption from speed limits of Police, traffic officers, and ambulance and fire brigade drivers
(1) Section 53 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, before the word bylaw, the words rule or.
(2) This subsection amended s 53(c) of the principal Act.

### 6 Definition of hazardous substance, etc
(1) This subsection amended s 70E(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted the definitions of the terms Hazardous substance enforcement officer, or enforcement officer, Operator, Premises, and Rail service operator in s 70E(1) of the principal Act.

### 7 Drivers to have licence with hazardous substance endorsement
This section inserted s 70H(2A) of the principal Act.

### 8 Power to stop road vehicles for breach of hazardous substances provision
(1) This subsection amended s 70I(1) of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection inserted s 70I(1A) of the principal Act.
(3) This subsection inserted s 70I(3) of the principal Act.

### 9 New sections inserted
This section inserted ss 70IA and 70IB of the principal Act.

### 10 Director and Commissioner of Police may appoint hazardous substance enforcement officers
This section inserted s 70K of the principal Act.

### 11 Bylaws as to use of roads
Section 72(1)(j) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word Fixing, and substituting the words In the case of Ministers of the Crown only, fixing.

### 12 Regulations

### 13 Schedules referring to offences may be amended by Order in Council
