# Ombudsmen Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Ombudsmen Act 1975

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Ombudsmen Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Ombudsmen Act 1975 (the principal Act).
(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 2 Protection and privileges of witnesses, etc
This section inserted s 19(5A) and (5B) of the principal Act.
