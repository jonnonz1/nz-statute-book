# Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 1997
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend The Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Armed Forces Discipline Amendment Act 1997 and is part of The Armed Forces Discipline Act 1971 (the principal Act).

### 2 Interpretation
This section amended s 2(1) of the principal Act by inserting the definition of the term Chief of Defence Force.

### 3 Transitional provisions
This section substituted the cross-heading above section 207, and repealed section 207 of the principal Act.

### 4 Technical amendments to principal Act
The principal Act is amended in the manner indicated in the Schedule.
