# Bail Amendment Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-12-16_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Bail Amendment Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Bail Act 2000.

### 4 Consideration of just cause for continued detention
(1) Section 8(1)(a) is amended by omitting real and significant.
(2) Section 8(3) is repealed.
