# Radiocommunications Amendment Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-03-26_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Radiocommunications Amendment Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Radiocommunications Act 1989.

### 4 Aggregation of current and successive management rights
