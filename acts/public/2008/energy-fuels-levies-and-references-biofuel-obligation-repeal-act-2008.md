# Energy (Fuels, Levies, and References) Biofuel Obligation Repeal Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-12-23_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Energy (Fuels, Levies, and References) Biofuel Obligation Repeal Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Energy (Fuels, Levies, and References) Act 1989.

## Part 1 — Repeal of biofuel obligation

### 4 Part 3A repealed
Part 3A is repealed.

## Part 2 — Consequential amendments to principal Act
