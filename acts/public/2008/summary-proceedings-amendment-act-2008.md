# Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-03-26_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Summary Proceedings Act 1957.

### 4 Schedule 1 amended
(1) Part 1 of Schedule 1 is amended by omitting the item relating to section 236 of the Crimes Act 1961 and substituting the following item:236(2)Assault with intent to rob
(2) Part 2 of Schedule 1 is amended by omitting the item relating to section 351 of the Gambling Act 2003.
