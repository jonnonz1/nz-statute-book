# Criminal Investigations (Bodily Samples) Amendment Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-03-26_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Criminal Investigations (Bodily Samples) Amendment Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Criminal Investigations (Bodily Samples) Act 1995.

### 4 Interpretation
Section 2(1) is amended by inserting the following definition in its appropriate alphabetical order:.
  - **video record** has the same meaning as in section 4 of the Evidence Act 2006

### 5 Consent to taking of bodily sample
(1) Section 9(1)(b) is amended by omitting videotape and substituting video record.
(2) Section 9(3) is amended by omitting videotape in each place where it appears and substituting in each case video record.

### 6 Consent to taking of buccal sample
(1) Section 24G(1)(b) is amended by omitting videotape and substituting video record.
(2) Section 24G(3) is amended by omitting videotape in each place where it appears and substituting in each case video record.

### 7 Consent to taking of bodily sample
(1) Section 34(1)(b) is amended by omitting videotape and substituting video record.
(2) Section 34(3) is amended by omitting videotape in each place where it appears and substituting in each case video record.
