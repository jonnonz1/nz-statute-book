# Biosecurity Amendment Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-03-26_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Biosecurity Amendment Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Biosecurity Act 1993.

### 4 Deputy chief technical officers
Section 102(2) is amended by inserting , including the power to appoint inspectors, authorised persons, and accredited persons after under this Act.
