# Victims' Rights Amendment Act 2008
_Consolidation as at 2008-06-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Victims' Rights Amendment Act 2008.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act amended
This Act amends the Victims' Rights Act 2002.

### 4 New section 16A inserted
The following section is inserted after section 16:Criminal proceedings to which section 16 does not apply
  Nothing in section 16 applies to a criminal proceeding if it is necessary to disclose the information in the charge in order to ensure that the defendant is fully and fairly informed of the nature of the charge.
