# Taxation Principles Reporting Act Repeal Act 2023
_Consolidation as at 2025-01-01_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Taxation Principles Reporting Act Repeal Act 2023.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after it receives the Royal assent.

## Part 1 — Repeal of this Act

### 3 Repeal of this Act
This Act is repealed on 1 January 2025.

## Part 2 — Repeal of Taxation Principles Reporting Act 2023

### 4 Repeal of Taxation Principles Reporting Act 2023
The Taxation Principles Reporting Act 2023 (2023 No 55) is repealed.

### 5 Consequential amendment
In schedule 1 of the Tax Administration Act 1994, delete Taxation Principles Reporting Act 2023.
