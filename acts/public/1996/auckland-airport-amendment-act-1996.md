# Auckland Airport Amendment Act 1996
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Auckland Airport Act 1987

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Auckland Airport Amendment Act 1996, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Auckland Airport Act 1987 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on a date to be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.

### 2 Interpretation
This section substituted the definition of the term Minister in s 2 of the principal Act.

### 3 Vesting of airport assets and airport liabilities in company
This section amended s 6(2)(d) of the principal Act.

### 4 Supply of information
This section amended s 13(1) of the principal Act.

### 5 References to Minister of Civil Aviation and Meteorological Services
Unless the context otherwise requires, every reference in—to the Minister of Civil Aviation and Meteorological Services shall be read as a reference to the Minister for State-Owned Enterprises.
