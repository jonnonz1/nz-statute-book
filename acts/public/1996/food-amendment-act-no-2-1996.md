# Food Amendment Act (No 2) 1996
_Consolidation as at 2016-03-01_

> An Act to amend the Food Act 1981

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Food Amendment Act (No 2) 1996, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Food Act 1981 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of July 1997.

### 2 Interpretation
This section inserted definitions of the terms Approved auditor, District, Enforcement authority, Exemption, Guideline and Territorial authority in s 2 of the principal Act.

### 3 New Part inserted
This section inserted Part 1A of the principal Act.

### 4 Prescribed foods
This section inserted section 11P(3) of the principal Act.
