# Law Reform Amendment Act 1996
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Law Reform Act 1936

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Law Reform Amendment Act 1996, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Law Reform Act 1936 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Effect of death on certain causes of action
(1) This subsection inserted s 3(2)(d) of the principal Act.
(2) Nothing in subsection (1) of this section shall affect any proceedings commenced before the date on which this section comes into force.
