# Summary Proceedings Amendment Act (No 2) 1996
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Summary Proceedings Act 1957

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act (No 2) 1996, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Summary Proceedings Act 1957 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Maximum penalty on summary conviction for indictable offence
(1) This section substituted section 7 of the principal Act.
(2) Section 5(1) of the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1980 is hereby consequentially repealed.

### 3 Defendant may plead guilty before or during preliminary hearing
(1) This subsection amended s 153A of the principal Act.
(2) This subsection amended s 153A of the principal Act.
(3) The following enactments are hereby consequentially repealed:
  (a)
  (b) Section 6 of the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1991.

### 4 If evidence sufficient defendant may be committed for trial or sentence
(1) This subsection substituted section 168(1)(b) of the principal Act.
(2) Section 168(1) of the principal Act is hereby amended—
  (a) By omitting from paragraph (a) the words Except where the offence is punishable by death,:
  (b) By omitting from paragraph (c) the words If the offence is punishable by death or.
(3) The following enactments are hereby consequentially repealed:
  (a) Section 11(3) of the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1980:
  (b) Section 17 of the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1995.
(4) The repeal, by subsection (3) of this section, of section 17 of the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1995 does not affect the validity of anything validated by subsection (2) of that section.

### 5 Certain hearings to be conducted by Judge
This section substituted s 185B(2) of the principal Act.
