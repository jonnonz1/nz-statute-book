# Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 2002
_Consolidation as at 2013-12-18_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Sale of Liquor Amendment Act 2002.
(2) In this Act, the Sale of Liquor Act 1989 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Variation, suspension, or cancellation of licences other than special licences
(1) Section 132(1)﻿(a) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the word Authority, the words or a District Licensing Agency.
(2) Section 132(6)﻿(a) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the word Authority, the words or a District Licensing Agency.
