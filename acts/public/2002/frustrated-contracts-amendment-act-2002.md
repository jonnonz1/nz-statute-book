# Frustrated Contracts Amendment Act 2002
_Consolidation as at 2017-09-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Frustrated Contracts Amendment Act 2002.
(2) In this Act, the Frustrated Contracts Act 1944 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 New section 2 substituted
The principal Act is amended by repealing section 2, and substituting the following section:Interpretation
  In this Act, Court means, in relation to any matter, the court, tribunal, or arbitral tribunal by or before which the matter falls to be determined.
