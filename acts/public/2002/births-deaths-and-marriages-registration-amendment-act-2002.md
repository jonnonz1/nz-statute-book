# Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Amendment Act 2002
_Consolidation as at 2023-06-15_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Amendment Act 2002.
(2) In this Act, the Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Act 1995 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.
