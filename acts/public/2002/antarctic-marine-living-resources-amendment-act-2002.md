# Antarctic Marine Living Resources Amendment Act 2002
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Antarctic Marine Living Resources Amendment Act 2002.
(2) In this Act, the Antarctic Marine Living Resources Act 1981 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 2(1) of the principal Act is amended by repealing paragraph (b) of the definition of New Zealand national, and substituting the following paragraph:
  (b) an individual who is ordinarily resident in New Zealand, as defined in section 2(2) and (2A) of the Overseas Investment Act 1973; or
