# Illegal Contracts Amendment Act 2002
_Consolidation as at 2017-09-01_

### 1 Title
(1) This Act is the Illegal Contracts Amendment Act 2002.
(2) In this Act, the Illegal Contracts Act 1970 is called the principal Act.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Interpretation
Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by repealing the definition of Court, and substituting the following definition:
  - **Court** means, in relation to any matter, the court, tribunal, or arbitral tribunal by or before which the matter falls to be determined

### 4 Illegal contract defined
Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words means any contract, the words governed by New Zealand law.

### 5 Court may grant relief
Section 7(3) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words under subsection (1) of this section, the words , and the nature and extent of any relief to be granted,.

### 6 Sections 9 and 9A repealed
The principal Act is amended by repealing sections 9 and 9A.
