# Social Security Amendment Act 1982
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Social Security Act 1964

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Social Security Amendment Act 1982 and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Social Security Act 1964 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 12 Beneficiaries in hospital
(1) This subsection substituted s 75 of the principal Act.
(2) The following enactments are hereby consequentially repealed
  (a) Section 24 of the Social Security Amendment Act 1976:
  (b) Section 11 of the Social Security Amendment Act 1981.
