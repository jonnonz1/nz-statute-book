# Fire Service Amendment Act 1982
_Consolidation as at 2017-07-01_

> An Act to amend the Fire Service Act 1975

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Fire Service Amendment Act 1982, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Fire Service Act 1975 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Levy
(1)
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) of this section, every levy due under section 48 of the principal Act which was due before the commencement of this Act and which was computed in accordance with subsection (5) of that section, as in force before the commencement of this Act, is hereby validated and deemed to have been lawfully made and in no case shall the amount of any such levy be increased by reason of the said subsection (1).
