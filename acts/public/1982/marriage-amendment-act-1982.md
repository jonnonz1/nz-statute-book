# Marriage Amendment Act 1982
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Marriage Act 1955

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Marriage Amendment Act 1982, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Marriage Act 1955 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
