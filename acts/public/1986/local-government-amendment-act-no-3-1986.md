# Local Government Amendment Act (No 3) 1986
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Local Government Act 1974

### 1 Short title
This Act may be cited as the Local Government Amendment Act (No 3) 1986, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Local Government Act 1974 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 15 Further amendments to Acts amended by Local Government Amendment Act 1985
(1)
(2) Part 2 of Schedule 3 to the Local Government Amendment Act 1985 is hereby amended as from its commencement by omitting the word Board from the references to the Hutt Valley Drainage Board Act 1967 and the Hutt Valley Drainage Board Amendment Act 1978.
(3) Part 3 of Schedule 3 to the Local Government Amendment Act 1985 is hereby amended as from its commencement by omitting from the item relating to the Soil Conservation and Rivers Control Act 1941 the expression Section 51, and substituting the expression Sections 13(9B) and 59.

### 16 Savings
The repeal by section 13 of the Local Government Amendment Act 1986 of section 8 of the Harbours Amendment Act 1977 and section 12(3) of the Electoral Amendment Act 1983 shall not affect the amendments made by those sections, and those amendments shall remain in force and continue to be of full effect.

### 17 Amendment relating to Local Elections and Polls Act 1976
Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Local Government Amendment Act 1986 is hereby amended as from the commencement of that Act by omitting from the item relating to the Local Elections and Polls Act 1976 the expression Subsections (4) and (5), and substituting the expression Subsection (4).
