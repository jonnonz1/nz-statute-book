# Estate and Gift Duties Amendment Act 1986
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Estate and Gift Duties Act 1968

### 1 Short Title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Estate and Gift Duties Amendment Act 1986, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Estate and Gift Duties Act 1968 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of July 1986 and apply to offences committed on or after that date.
(3) Any proceedings commenced (whether before or after the commencement of this Act) for an offence committed before the commencement of this Act shall be heard and determined as if this Act had not been enacted.
