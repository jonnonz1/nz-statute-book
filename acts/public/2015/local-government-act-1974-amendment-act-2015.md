# Local Government Act 1974 Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Local Government Act 1974 Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Local Government Act 1974 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 342 amended (Stopping and closing of roads)
In section 342(1)(a), delete (not being a borough council).

### 5 Schedule 10 amended
In Schedule 10, clause 11, replace Ministry of Transport with New Zealand Transport Agency.
