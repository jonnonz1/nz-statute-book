# Pork Industry Board Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Pork Industry Board Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Pork Industry Board Act 1997 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 45 amended (Warrant to inspect place other than place of business)
(1) In section 45(1) and (2), replace to the authorised person with to every authorised person.
(2) In section 45(1) and (2), delete authorising the person.
