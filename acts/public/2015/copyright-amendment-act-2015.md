# Copyright Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Copyright Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Copyright Act 1994 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 206 amended (Membership of Tribunal)
(1) Replace section 206(1) with:
  (1) The Tribunal consists of a chairperson and at least 2, but not more than 5, other persons.
(2) In section 206(3), delete 5.
