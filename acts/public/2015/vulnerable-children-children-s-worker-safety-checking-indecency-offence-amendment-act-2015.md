# Vulnerable Children (Children’s Worker Safety Checking—Indecency Offence) Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-04-09_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Vulnerable Children (Children’s Worker Safety Checking—Indecency Offence) Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 28th day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Vulnerable Children Act 2014 (the principal Act).
