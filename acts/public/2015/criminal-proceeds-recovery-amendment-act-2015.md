# Criminal Proceeds (Recovery) Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-11-06_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Criminal Proceeds (Recovery) Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Criminal Proceeds (Recovery) Act 2009 (the principal Act).
