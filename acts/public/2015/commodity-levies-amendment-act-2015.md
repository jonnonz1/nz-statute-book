# Commodity Levies Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Commodity Levies Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Commodity Levies Act 1990 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 19 amended (Power of search)
In section 19(1), replace authorise the constable or person with authorise every constable and designated person.
