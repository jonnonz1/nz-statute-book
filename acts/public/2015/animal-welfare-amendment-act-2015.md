# Animal Welfare Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Animal Welfare Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Animal Welfare Act 1999 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 131 amended (Search warrants)
In section 131(1), replace authorise the constable or the inspector with authorise every constable and inspector.
