# International Finance Agreements Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-12-11_

### 1 Title
This Act is the International Finance Agreements Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the International Finance Agreements Act 1961 (the principal Act).

## Part 1 — Preliminary provisions

### 4 Section 1A amended (Purpose)
After section 1A(4), insert:
  (5) A further purpose, implemented by the International Finance Agreements Amendment Act 2015, is to enable the Government of New Zealand to be a member of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank.

### 5 Section 2 amended (Interpretation)
In section 2, insert in their appropriate alphabetical order:
  - **Infrastructure Bank** means the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank
  - **Infrastructure Bank Agreement** means the Articles of Agreement set out in Schedule 8, providing for the establishment and operation of the Infrastructure Bank

## Part 2 — Membership of Infrastructure Bank

### 6 Section 3 amended (Membership of Fund, Bank, Corporation, and Agency)
(1) In the heading to section 3, replace and Agency with Agency, and Infrastructure Bank.
(2) After section 3(2), insert:
  (3) Approval is also given for the New Zealand Government to become a member of the Infrastructure Bank in accordance with the Infrastructure Bank Agreement.

### 7 Section 4 amended (Reserve Bank of New Zealand to be depository)
In section 4(1), replace and the Agency with the Agency, and the Infrastructure Bank.

### 8 Section 5 amended (Payments under Agreements and Convention)
(1) In section 5(c) and (d), replace . with :.
(2) After section 5(d), insert:
  (e) all payments required to be made from time to time to the Infrastructure Bank under the terms of the Infrastructure Bank Agreement.
(3) In section 5, insert as subsection (2):
  (2) Expenses or capital expenditure may be incurred without further appropriation than this section for the purpose of the payments authorised by subsection (1) (see sections 4 and 11 of the Public Finance Act 1989).
