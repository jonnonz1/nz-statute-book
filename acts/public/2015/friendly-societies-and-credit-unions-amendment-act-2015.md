# Friendly Societies and Credit Unions Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Friendly Societies and Credit Unions Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Friendly Societies and Credit Unions Act 1982 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 10 repealed (Annual report)
Repeal section 10.
