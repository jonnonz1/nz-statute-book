# Limited Partnerships Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-11-06_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Limited Partnerships Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Limited Partnerships Act 2008 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 79 amended (Disclosure of information and reports)
In section 79(1B), definition of law enforcement purposes, replace paragraph (b)﻿(v) with:
  (v) any offence that is punishable by imprisonment for a term of 5 years or more and includes any act, wherever committed, that if committed in New Zealand would constitute an offence punishable by imprisonment for a term of 5 years or more:
