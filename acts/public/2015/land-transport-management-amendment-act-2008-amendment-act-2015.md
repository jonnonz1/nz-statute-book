# Land Transport Management Amendment Act 2008 Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-03-25_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Land Transport Management Amendment Act 2008 Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Land Transport Management Amendment Act 2008 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 47 amended (Consequences of establishment of New Zealand Transport Agency)
In section 47(1), replace 1 July 2008 with 1 August 2008.
