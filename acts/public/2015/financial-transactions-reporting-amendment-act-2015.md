# Financial Transactions Reporting Amendment Act 2015
_Consolidation as at 2015-11-06_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Financial Transactions Reporting Amendment Act 2015.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 1996 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 21 amended (Protection of identity of persons making suspicious transaction reports)
(1) In section 21(2)﻿(a)﻿(ii), replace any serious offence (within the meaning of section 243 of the Crimes Act 1961) with an offence within the meaning of section 243 of the Crimes Act 1961.
(2) In section 21(2)﻿(a)﻿(ii), replace proceeds of that serious offence with proceeds of that offence.

### 5 Section 28 amended (Application of Privacy Act 1993)
(1) In section 28(b)﻿(ii), replace any serious offence (within the meaning of section 243 of the Crimes Act 1961) with an offence within the meaning of section 243 of the Crimes Act 1961.
(2) In section 28(b)﻿(ii), replace proceeds of that serious offence with proceeds of that offence.
