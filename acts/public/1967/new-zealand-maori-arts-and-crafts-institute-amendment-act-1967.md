# New Zealand Maori Arts and Crafts Institute Amendment Act 1967
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Rotorua Maori Arts and Crafts Institute Act 1963

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the New Zealand Maori Arts and Crafts Institute Amendment Act 1967, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Act heretofore known as the Rotorua Maori Arts and Crafts Institute Act 1963 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Change of name
(1) The principal Act may hereafter be cited as the New Zealand Maori Arts and Crafts Institute Act 1963.
