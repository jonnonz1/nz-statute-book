# Land Amendment Act 1967
_Consolidation as at 2007-09-03_

> An Act to amend the Land Act 1948

### 1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Land Amendment Act 1967, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Land Act 1948 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

### 2 Registration of dealings with certain leases and licences
(1) This subsection substituted sections 83(2) and (3), and inserted section 83(3A), of the principal Act.
(2) Every instrument or notice which has been registered before the commencement of this section and would have been registrable if this section had been in force shall be deemed to have been lawfully registered.
(3) Section 3 of the Land Amendment Act 1961 is hereby amended by repealing paragraphs (b) and (c) of subsection (2).
