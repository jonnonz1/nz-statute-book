# Local Government (Rating) Amendment Act 2016
_Consolidation as at 2016-10-17_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Local Government (Rating) Amendment Act 2016.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 March 2017.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Local Government (Rating) Act 2002 (the principal Act).

### 4 Section 70 amended (Rating unit may be sold or leased)
After section 70(2), insert:
  (3) The Registrar may charge the fee, and require the fee to be paid, before the work required of the Registrar in effecting the sale or lease is begun.

### 5 Section 75 amended (Application of proceeds of rating sale or lease)
In section 75(a), after fee, insert (unless the fee has already been paid).
