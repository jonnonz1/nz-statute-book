# Bills of Exchange Amendment Act 2016
_Consolidation as at 2016-10-17_

### 1 Title
This Act is the Bills of Exchange Amendment Act 2016.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on 1 March 2017.

### 3 Principal Act
This Act amends the Bills of Exchange Act 1908 (the principal Act).

### 4 New section 96A inserted (Actions on other lost negotiable instruments)
After section 96, insert:Actions on other lost negotiable instruments1908 No 89 s 88; 1947 No 16 s 118
  Section 70 applies to a negotiable instrument that is not a bill in the same way as it applies to a bill.
