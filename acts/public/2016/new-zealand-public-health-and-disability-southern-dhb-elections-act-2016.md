# New Zealand Public Health and Disability (Southern DHB) Elections Act 2016
_Consolidation as at 2016-05-13_

### 1 Title
This Act is the New Zealand Public Health and Disability (Southern DHB) Elections Act 2016.

### 2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

### 3 Repeal
This Act is repealed on the day that elected members of the Southern DHB come into office after the triennial general election of 2019, in accordance with clause 14 of Schedule 2 of the 2000 Act.

**Preliminary provisions**

### 4 Purpose
The purpose of this Act is to cancel the 2016 triennial general election of the Southern DHB in order to provide, until the repeal date, for the continuation of the term of office of a commissioner for the Southern DHB.

### 5 Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—
  - **2000 Act** means the New Zealand Public Health and Disability Act 2000
  - **commissioner** means a person appointed under section 31 of the 2000 Act to be a commissioner for the Southern DHB
  - **repeal date** means the day specified for the repeal of this Act by section 3
  - **Southern DHB** means the district health board of that name listed in Schedule 1 of the 2000 Act
  - **triennial general election** has the meaning given in clause 1 of Schedule 2 of the 2000 Act.

### 6 Act binds the Crown
This Act binds the Crown.

**Cancellation of election**

### 7 Cancellation of 2016 election of Southern DHB
(1) The triennial general election for the Southern DHB in 2016 is cancelled.
(2) The following provisions of the 2000 Act do not apply to the Southern DHB in relation to the triennial general election of 2016:
  (a) clause 8 of Schedule 2 (which provides for the application of the provisions of the Local Electoral Act 2001 and regulations made under that Act to every election of a DHB, subject to the provisions of the 2000 Act and to any regulations made under that Act); and
  (b) clause 9 of Schedule 2 (which requires the election of all DHBs to be held in conjunction with every triennial general election); and
  (c) section 7(f) of the Local Electoral Act 2001 (which applies that Act to every election of a DHB).
